In this message a guest speaker at Vine Cluster Church reads Esther 1–2 as a picture of the old and new covenants. He says Queen Vashti, who refused the king, pictures both Israel under the old covenant and a refusing church today, while Esther pictures the new wife who asks how to please the King. He closes by describing the church as the bride of Christ who makes herself ready, and an invitation to accept Jesus.
