🎙From the sermonDid They Live It? · Pastor David G. Kincer▶ 0:00

Pastor David G. Kincer asks whether the apostles lived up to Romans 8—nothing can separate us—and shows from Paul’s sufferings that they did. He gives five reasons: a personal encounter with Jesus, Jesus praying for them, ongoing conversion, opened understanding and the power of the Holy Ghost. With the word and the gifts of the Spirit they turned a hostile world upside down, and he says the church can do the same in America.
