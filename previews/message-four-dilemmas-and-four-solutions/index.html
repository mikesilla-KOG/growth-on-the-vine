In this message Pastor David G. Kincer walks through Isaiah 36–39 and four dilemmas Hezekiah faced: the Assyrian army, the word from God that he would die, a sickness, and the prophecy that his sons would be taken. He teaches that prayer, humility and repentance act as a “higher law” that can change even a God-given prophecy, and that Hezekiah’s failure to pray about the fourth dilemma left it to his son Manasseh. He closes with the power of the gospel and standing in the gap in prayer.
