Continuing the Burning Bush series, the message explains God’s new covenant in Ezekiel 36: a cleansing, a new heart and His Spirit. Like a body, the spiritual man needs a working heart, the life-giving blood of Christ, and the breath of the Spirit that comes by hearing His word; Saul and Lazarus show what happens when we hear and respond.
