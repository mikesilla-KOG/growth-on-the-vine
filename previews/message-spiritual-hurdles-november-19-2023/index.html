A church member filling in teaches from the parable of the talents that the servant hid his talent because his perception of his master was wrong. God is not a hard master: He numbers our hairs, delights to give us the kingdom and casts our sins into the sea. The hurdles to overcome are hypocrisy, bad choices and stunted doctrine, and the cure is the simplicity in Christ and His living word and Spirit.
