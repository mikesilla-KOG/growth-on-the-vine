In “God’s Correction Tactics Part 2,” Pastor David G. Kincer says God’s correction comes by his Word and, when it is ignored, by punishment, and that the goal is the peaceable fruit of righteousness (Hebrews 12). He uses Ezekiel 14, Isaiah 29:6 and 1 Corinthians 11:30, and Ezra’s request to God as a pattern for accepting correction and asking God to keep his promises. He closes with Proverbs 13:24 and 19:18 on prompt discipline.
