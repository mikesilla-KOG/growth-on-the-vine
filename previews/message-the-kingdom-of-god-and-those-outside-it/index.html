What is “given” to those who know the mystery of the kingdom?

▶ Watch this part (0:01)

Short answer: From Mark 4:11 and its parallels (Matthew 13, Acts 28, Isaiah 6) he says knowledge of the mystery of the kingdom of God is given—not acquired or paid for. A mystery is something hidden you must search for, and the kingdom is the dominion ruled by God; those without that knowledge are outside the kingdom.

Why did Jesus speak to outsiders in parables?

▶ Watch this part (2:21)

Short answer: He says a parable is a story illustrating a spiritual truth—like the treasure in the field, which is mankind that Jesus bought by dying on the cross—and the Old Testament shadows were parables of Christ. Outsiders have closed their own eyes and ears, so they see the parables but don’t perceive.
