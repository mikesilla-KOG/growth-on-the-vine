In this third message on six of the seven seals, Pastor David G. Kincer finishes the sixth seal, connecting it to Joel in Acts 2:16, Matthew 24:29 and Isaiah 34:4. He then previews the seventh seal with the sealing of God’s servants in Revelation 7, teaches that believers are sealed by the Holy Spirit, and gives reasons for a delay in the second coming: the martyr number, the sealing, and the great harvest of the multitude no one could count.
