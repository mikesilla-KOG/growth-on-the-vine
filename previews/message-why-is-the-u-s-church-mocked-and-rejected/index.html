What were America’s great spiritual awakenings?

▶ Watch this part (0:00)

Short answer: He asks why the U.S. church is mocked and rejected and says history records four great awakenings, the first in 1730 before the Revolutionary War and the second from 1800 to 1840, which addressed alcohol, tobacco, slavery and women’s rights.

🎙From the sermonTranscript of this part · Pastor David G. Kincer

▶ 0:00 And we thank you and appreciate you joining us in further pursuit of clarity on crucial choices. Today we’re going to ask the question: has the United States of America church made the best choices? Or, why is the United States church mocked and rejected today? Now, we know from history there have been four great spiritual awakenings. The first was in 1730, preceding the Revolutionary War and preparing us for it. The second was in 1800 through 1840, where alcohol, tobacco, the abolition of slavery and women’s rights was addressed.

Why does the church keep needing revival?

▶ Watch this part (0:52)

Short answer: He describes the third and fourth awakenings and says that when you go to sleep you have to be reawakened. From Matthew 13:25 he says that while men slept the enemy sowed tares among the wheat, and that is what happens when the church goes to sleep.

🎙From the sermonTranscript of this part · Pastor David G. Kincer

▶ 0:52 The third one was 1850 through the 1900s, and pre- and post-Civil War, and they address the social ills of the drunks, the orphans, the widows and so forth. The fourth was in 1948 through ‘80, where the Holy Spirit was poured out, miracles happened, praise and worship was introduced to the church, unity. And in the, the secular world, In God We Trust was put on our money in 1954, and One Nation Under God was put in our pledge in 1956, and throughout the ‘50s millions of Americans made a decision to serve Jesus Christ. Now, why do we need four awakenings? Well, when you go to sleep, you have to be reawakened, either by yourself naturally or an alarm. In Matthew 13:25 Jesus said, while men slept, his enemies came and sowed tares among the wheat. So the children of the wicked one, the children of Satan, among the wheat, while men slept. And so that’s what happens when the church goes to sleep: we need an awakening. In the ‘60s, Bible, prayer and Jesus was put out of public places and public schools; the sexual revolution and evolution was replacing them.
