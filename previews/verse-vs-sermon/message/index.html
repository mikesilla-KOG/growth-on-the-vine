Is the Old Testament still for us today?

▶ Watch this part (0:00)

Short answer: He says everything written in the Bible was written for us to learn by, so the Old Testament Scriptures are very important to us. When God dealt with a nation, or a nation fell, we need to learn why and how to turn it around. He says the Old Testament events “occurred” and were not myths, and that “All Scripture” is profitable, with righteousness and good works as the focus of the Bible.

🎙From the sermonTranscript of this part · Pastor David G. Kincer

▶ 0:00 Everything written in the Bible was written for us to learn by. So Old Testament Scriptures are very important to us. All the Scriptures are important to us. And to remind you of this, Romans 15:4 says, “Whatsoever things were written aforetime were written for our learning, that we, through patience and comfort of the Scriptures, might have hope.” So everything in the Bible is for us to go back and learn by. When God dealt with a nation, or a nation fell, or whatever, we need to learn why they did that and how to turn it around.

▶ 0:45 1 Corinthians 10:11 says, “All these things happened.” Now, not like Bill O’Reilly on Fox News, who says these were allegories, fictitious stories, myths, okay? That’s what Schuler [unclear] also taught, that the Bible was just a bunch of allegories and myths. But it says here, all these things, what? They occurred, okay? They weren’t myths, they weren’t dreams, they weren’t fantasies. They were actually things that occurred to them for examples, and written for our admonition, upon whom the ends of the earth are come. And we know that the end of the world started when Jesus died on the cross and ascended back into heaven and started his church. We’re in the last days of God’s calendar. We don’t know how many years that’s going to be, but we’re in the ends, and this is for our learning. You got it?

▶ 1:56 Now, there are people that say the Old Testament Scriptures are outdated, you know, it doesn’t work. They’re not reading their Bible. You learn tremendous things from the whole book, all the Scriptures, all right? And then 2 Timothy 3:16, “All Scripture” — how many? All of them — “is profitable for reproof, correction, instruction in” what? Righteousness. It’s righteousness that’s the focus, folks. I keep telling you that, all right? “That the man of God may be perfect, thoroughly furnished unto all” what? Good works.

▶ 2:32 So we’re supposed to do what? Good works. These people that say, “It’s not by works, no matter what you do, if you believe in Christ you’re going” — no. The focus of the Bible is to turn you from bad works to good works. And this is one of the important things that the baptism of the Holy Ghost does: the Holy Spirit turns you to good works. And we need that baptism of the Holy Spirit.
