What is the real goal of God’s Word?

The goal of the commandment is love out of a pure heart. Gifts and religious activity are means — not the destination.

Key Takeaway

Worship, prayer, and gifts are means — love from a pure heart is the goal.

📖Scripture (NKJV)

Key Scripture

1 Timothy 1:5 — Now the purpose of the commandment is love from a pure heart, from a good conscience, and from sincere faith.

Paul sums it up in 1 Timothy 1:5. The word purpose means the goal.

📖Scripture (NKJV)

Now the purpose of the commandment is love from a pure heart, from a good conscience, and from sincere faith.

— 1 Timothy 1:5

We think the goal is eternal life, or the gifts of the Spirit, or activity that looks spiritual. Paul says the goal is love — and God distributes gifts as He wills. Gifts are means. Love is the goal.

Ask yourself: Am I nailing the means — and missing the goal?

📖Scripture (NKJV)

Plain language: worship, prayer, gifts, and rituals can be good means — but love from a pure heart is the destination.
