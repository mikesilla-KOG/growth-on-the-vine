In this message Pastor David G. Kincer sets Jeremiah 31 and 32 in the middle of Israel’s captivity and shows the hope God gave there: a new, everlasting covenant in which God puts His Word in our hearts, forgives, and promises to do us good. He then lays out our part, to repent, believe the gospel, confess Jesus as Lord and receive Him, and uses the butterfly and 1 John 2 to describe growing from baby to child to young man to father in Christ.

What was happening in Israel when Jeremiah 31 and 32 were written?

▶ Watch this part (0:00)

Short answer: He says Josiah was the last good king, but his son “burned the Scriptures” and did evil before God, the first captivity came, and later the king of Babylon burned Jerusalem and destroyed the temple.
