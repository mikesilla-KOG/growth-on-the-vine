Why do people call the gospel foolishness?

▶ Watch this part (0:00)

Short answer: He says Jesus is a stumbling block to the Jews because they wanted a sign, and to the Gentiles it is “foolishness because they’re looking for total intellect,” just human reasoning rather than spiritual or scriptural reasoning. He says the preaching of Jesus Christ, the gospel and the cross is what saved him: “He died for me.”

Transcript of this part

▶ 0:00 So, where’s the power of God and the preaching of Jesus Christ, the Gospel, and the cross? And folks, that’s what saved me. He convicted me. He died for me. If he died for me to forgive me, then I owe him something. I have a debt to show my appreciation by what? Living for him. And if I fall, I get back up, admit it, and keep going. The Jews require a sign, and the Gentiles [unclear] intellect, and the Jews see Jesus as a stumbling block because they want a sign, and he didn’t give them any sign but the death and burial and resurrection. So Jesus is a stumbling block to the Jews who have their own opinion on how this is going to work out. And the Gentiles, it’s foolishness because they’re looking for total intellect, just total human reasoning, not spiritual reasoning, not scriptural reasoning, human reasoning. And so they go, oh, this is nonsense. How can anybody be born of a virgin? How can anybody, you know, be raised from the dead?

Does it take great speaking to change lives?

▶ Watch this part (2:52)

The only demonstration of the Spirit and power we have seen so far, he says, is “salvation and changed lives.”

▶ 16:44 I had a tragic come in my life and somebody says, man, you know, do you pray and what do you do? I said, I just do what I always do. I just live my life.
