🎙From the sermonHow to Meet with Jesus · Pastor David G. Kincer▶ 0:00

Pastor David G. Kincer establishes that Jesus died for the world and for the church that sins, and invites everyone to come to him for rest, free of man-made religious burdens. Using the tabernacle, he shows we enter God’s presence boldly by the blood and come in with thanksgiving and praise, which God inhabits. Believers are the chosen generation created to praise him, and meeting God strengthens and compels us.
