Where were the seven churches of Asia, and where is Patmos?

▶ Watch this part (0:00)

Short answer: He uses maps to show that “the province of Asia” was a province of the Roman Empire, near the Mediterranean and Black Seas, and that John was “banished to the island of Patmos” because of preaching “the word and the testimony of Christ.” He says that when you really stand up for Christ, “people are not going to like you.”

I didn’t have it labeled, and that red dot up here above Africa is the province of Asia in the Roman Empire. During the Roman Empire, our time, they had a province there called Asia. Why was John put on Patmos, and were the letters only for seven churches?

▶ Watch this part (2:54)

Short answer: He says John was put in a pot of boiling oil and “it didn’t kill John,” so they banished him to Patmos. He says there were more than seven churches in Asia (such as those addressed in Colossians and Galatians), but because God is not “a respecter of persons” the messages were “for all his servants,” written “directly to you.”

What do “grace” and “peace” mean in Revelation 1:4?

▶ Watch this part (5:36)

Short answer: He says grace “means favor, divine influence,” so being under grace means being under “his divine influence,” which should show in how we dress, talk and live. He says peace is “the absence of a struggle,” and that God’s peace settles conflicts over right and wrong because “God has his own traditions, God has his own culture.”

All right, we today express the nature of what we give ourselves to: our hairstyle, our clothing, our tattoos, our nose rings, our way we talk is all reflections from the music, the movies, the TV, the reading material and the friends we run with.
