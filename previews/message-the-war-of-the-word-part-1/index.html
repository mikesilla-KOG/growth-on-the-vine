How many wars are there in the book of Revelation?

▶ Watch this part (0:01)

Short answer: He says there are “13 wars, 13 conflicts” in Revelation and walks through them: the seals, the locusts, the beast fighting the two witnesses, Michael and his angels fighting Satan, Satan warring with the saints, the beast out of the sea, Armageddon, and the beast and the horns warring with the Lord. He says it helps to see “the whole picture.”
