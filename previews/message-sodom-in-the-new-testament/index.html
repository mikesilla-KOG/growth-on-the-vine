Short answer: He lays out a timeline in which about 400 years separate the flood, the judgment of Sodom, the judgment of Egypt, the period of the judges, the era of the kings, and then 400 silent years before Jesus came. He says Jesus did not follow the pattern for His return “cuz we’re 2,000 years from that time,” but he says judgment is surely coming.
