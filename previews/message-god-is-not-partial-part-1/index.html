What does it mean that God is not partial?

▶ Watch this part (0:01)

Short answer: He says there is “no favoritism no respect or persons with God,” so what God did with Abraham he will do with us. He says we sometimes think others are better, more gifted, or that we are too bad to be changed, but even the unpardonable sin “was forgiven by the blood of Christ,” and spiritual education is open to everyone.

🎙From the sermonTranscript of this part · Pastor David G. Kincer

▶ 0:01 We found today, we’re going to find that there is no partiality with God. God does not favor one above another. Absolutely no favoritism, no respect or persons with God. If God did things with Abraham, he will do things with us. Abraham’s faith works. But we got to get away from the faith that America is preaching to us and get back to the faith that Abraham had. Sometimes we think that some people are just made different. That’s why they’re better than us, and, and you know, they attain more than us. Sometimes we think other people are more gifted than us, and we excuse ourselves from obtaining these things. Sometimes we think we’re just too bad to be changed. God just can’t do it, we’re just too bad, we’ve been too bad. And I’ve showed you over and over that the unpardonable sin was forgiven by the blood of Christ, and so anything can be forgiven, and any of us can be forgiven. Amen.

▶ 1:30 Sometimes we think we’re uneducated. You know, some of us could have sought a better education, but it’s not secular education that gets this stuff done, it’s spiritual education, and all of us can get spiritual education. Amen. We don’t have to be a doctor, a lawyer or a PhD. And sometimes we think God just favors other people, and uh, he’s just special because God just favored him, and that’s why the other people make great Christians and I will barely make it into heaven, if at all. That’s a lot of mentality out there, and we got to drop that and remember that God is not partial. He doesn’t come to one above another. He offers us all the same thing. Could that be true? It’s what we do with the offer that makes the difference. And hopefully today we can see that this faith of Abraham is the ingredient that all of us can have to get these promises.

What is the faith of Abraham? (Romans 4:21)

▶ Watch this part (2:45)

Short answer: He says Abraham “was fully persuaded” that God was able to perform what He promised, and it was counted to him for righteousness. He says we can be righteous by believing like Abraham that God has the power to do what He promised, even if we do not yet see the result, and that this faith gives “great confidence.”

Now, if you want to be righteous before you ever obtain living a life of righteousness, then the key here is you got to believe like Abraham did, that God has the power to perform what he promised us. And I may not see the result in my life yet, but I see that God has the power to do for me what he promised to do, and that makes me righteous. Hallelujah. Why does he say the faith some preachers promote is not the Bible?

▶ Watch this part (4:26)

Short answer: He says some preachers say “if you believe, God will do this for you,” and then blame the person when it does not happen, but “in the Bible there is no excuse” and “God doesn’t have to heal everybody.” He says God does what He wants, but He will heal, and the promise depends on God’s power, not the minister.

🎙From the sermonTranscript of this part · Pastor David G. Kincer

▶ 4:26 Now, this is important stuff. This is your foundational stuff to grow and mature in God and never quit and never give up. This is the real deal, and it’s that simple. It’s not this faith that they’re promoting, and all these preachers, they said, “Well, if you believe, God will do this for you.” You know, God did a lot of things for people that did not believe. See, our faith has to rise above their lack of faith, or else we just give an excuse: “Well, you know, you didn’t get your healing because you didn’t believe.” Well, that’s a great excuse, but it’s not Bible. God doesn’t have to heal everybody. God does what he wants in heaven and earth, but he will heal, he will do things. Amen. And so these preachers of this kind of faith, they always have an outlet: it’s your fault because you didn’t believe. But in the Bible there is no excuse. If you have the faith of Abraham, God will give you the promise, and it’s not the minister, now, it is God who has the power to do what he said he would do. Did that make sense to you?
