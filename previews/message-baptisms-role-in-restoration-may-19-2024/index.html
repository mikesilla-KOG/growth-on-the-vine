On a baptism Sunday the message explains what water baptism includes: confessing Jesus publicly, believing all His commandments are truth, and fully submerging in repentance. Baptism is burial with Christ and resurrection to new life, the sign of the new covenant as circumcision was for Abraham, and a commitment that God will give a tender new heart.
