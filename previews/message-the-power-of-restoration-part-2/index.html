Does sin bring correction into our lives?

▶ Watch this part (0:00)

Short answer: He says “a lot of things you do in life are going to bring correction,” not just sex and alcohol: steal or run with a gang and “you’re going to wind up in jail.” He says “that’s how sin corrects you,” and that most people don’t take the correction, “they just get harder.”

You want to hang out with a gang, you’re going to wind up in jail, you’re going to destroy your life. Are you with me? See, that’s how sin corrects you. ▶ Watch this part (1:26)

Short answer: He says the passage has parents taking a stubborn, rebellious son who “will not obey our voice” to the city officials, and tells young people that parents who are “any kind of parents at all” will draw the line and correct them. He tells parents that if they live a loose life and want their kids to do right, “they’re going to know you’re a hypocrite.”

▶ Watch this part (6:29)

Short answer: He says ashes can’t be made into anything, but God says “my restoration is so powerful” that even if you have destroyed your life and there is no sign of life in you, “I can make your life beautiful … but better than before.” He says “we don’t need psychology, we need the preaching of the word and the move of the Spirit,” and that the oil of joy means God gives joy “right where you’re at.”

🎙From the sermonTranscript of this part · Pastor David G. Kincer

▶ 6:29 “To comfort and console those who mourn, to give them beauty for ashes.” Now here we go. What can we make out of ashes? Nothing. There is nothing. There, there’s nothing we can do. Now, this partly burnt piece of wood, we could use it for something, even if it was just a stake in the ground, it’s usable. But the ashes are gone. And God says, what, my restoration is so powerful, young people, listen to me, my restoration is so powerful that if you have destroyed your life and there’s not a sign of life in you, I can make your life beautiful, not just like I, not just like it was before, because you weren’t beautiful before, but better than before. I’ll make your life look like mine. Beautiful. Hallelujah.

▶ 7:26 Now, that’s our promise. We, we don’t need psychology, we need the preaching of the word and the move of the Spirit in His church. How many would like beauty for ashes? You know, maybe we’re just partly burned, but you want to be beautified back. Amen. “The oil of joy for mourning.” Oh, you had a rough home and a rough life, and oh man, your mom and dad were this and your mom and dad were that, and the neighborhood you lived in was this and the neighborhood you lived in was that, and it just depressed you and caused sorrow. God says, I’ll give you joy. I won’t even take you out of the thing, I’ll give you joy right where you’re at, then I’ll take you out. It’s easy to lead someone that can smile.
