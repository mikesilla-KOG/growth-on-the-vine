What is the river of life in the church, and how is knowledge different from the tree of life? (Revelation 22:1)

▶ Watch this part (0:01)

Short answer: He says “you are the temple of the living God,” so “when we come together all of our streams come together and make one big river.” He says Adam and Eve’s mistake was thinking knowledge was enough: “the knowledge is got to bring us to the life,” and the church should be “known for the river of life flowing in it.”
