🎙From the sermonFor Our Example · Pastor David G. Kincer▶ 0:00

In this message Pastor David G. Kincer walks through Jeremiah to show what God told His prophet to preach: root out evil, plant the right seed, and do not be afraid. He explains the broken cisterns of Jeremiah 2:13, the three sources of opposition (civil government, temple leaders and church members), and how the captivities came while false prophets promised victory. He applies it to America today, urging Christians to amend their ways, humble themselves and pray, and to have compassion on those who oppose them.

What did Jeremiah say was wrong with the prophets of Jerusalem? (Jeremiah 23:14)

▶ Watch this part (0:01)

Short answer: He reads Jeremiah 23:14, where the prophets commit adultery, walk in lies, and “strengthen the hands of evildoers” so that none returns from wickedness. He says this is what Sodom and Gomorrah did, “they held no one accountable,” and that when the gospel of repentance isn’t preached “we are strengthening their hand to live in sin.”
