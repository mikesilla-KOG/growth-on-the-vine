In “Adding,” a Vine Cluster guest speaker (who refers to Mr. Kincer in the third person) walks through 2 Peter 1:5-10 and says believers must add virtue, knowledge, self-control, patience, godliness, brotherly kindness and agape love to their faith so they will not be barren. He contrasts Daniel, Shadrach, Meshach and Abednego—who kept adding and were found ten times better—with Solomon, who received great wisdom but threw out virtue and fell. He closes with the promise that if you add to your faith you will never fall and will receive a rich welcome into the eternal kingdom.
