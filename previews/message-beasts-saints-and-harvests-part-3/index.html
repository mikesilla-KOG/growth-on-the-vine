Who are the 144,000 with the Lamb on Mount Zion? (Revelation 14:1, 3:12)

▶ Watch this part (0:00)

Short answer: He says “Zion is the church,” so the 144,000 with the Lamb are the people of God, and that he has “no idea what 144,000 is” beyond 12 times 12. Reading Revelation 3:12, he says the overcomer gets God’s name written on him, that we overcome “by our faith in Jesus Christ,” and “we have the name of God written on us” if we pursue God as overcomers.
