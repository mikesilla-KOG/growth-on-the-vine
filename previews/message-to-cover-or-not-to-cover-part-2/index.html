Were both men and women made in the image of God?

▶ Watch this part (0:00)

Short answer: He says Genesis 1:26-28 shows both were made in the image of God and given dominion, and that Paul knows this but is “concentrating on these male egos.” God said “let us,” not “I will,” so the Godhead is plural, and “mankind” means the human race, not male. He adds that the woman was given the same blessing, “but Paul left that part out.”

🎙From the sermonTranscript of this part · Pastor David G. Kincer

▶ 0:00 Genesis 1:26, we got to make it clear that both were made in the image of God. See, Paul knows this, but he didn’t bring out the fact that the woman was made in the image of God also. See, Paul knows this, but he didn’t bring it out. Why? He is concentrating on these male egos that he is going to bury here in a couple of verses. “Let us make man in our image.” There’s the plural: “let us.” God didn’t say “I will,” He said “let us.” The Godhead is plural. Do you understand that? Jesus was back there at the beginning. “Let us make man,” mankind; it’s not man, male; it’s mankind. A lot of words for man in the Bible is mankind, the human race. What does “power on her head because of the angels” mean in 1 Corinthians 11:10?

▶ Watch this part (2:43)

Short answer: He says nowhere in the Bible does it say a woman has to have power over her head because of the angels, and that several translations say “power” is the sign of the husband’s authority, which he calls “a big mistake.” He says the word means real supernatural power, as in Matthew 28:18 and Luke 10:19, and that angels are spirit beings who do not need a covering, while Satan acts like an angel of light.
