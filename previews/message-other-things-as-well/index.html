In this message Pastor David G. Kincer teaches from 2 Peter 1:3-4 that believers are called to be partakers of the divine nature. He traces the first promise in Genesis 3:15, the woman’s seed bruising Satan’s head, and says Jesus and His church have rendered Satan powerless. He then uses the parable of the wheat and the tares in Matthew 13 to explain why the good and bad seed grow together, and how behavior shows who are children of God and who are children of the devil.
