Do we only want to hear smooth things?

💡 Short answerMany people do. They want to be told they’re okay, so they hire ministers to say what they want to hear instead of what is right.

🔎 WhyPastor Kincer cites Isaiah 30:10, “Speak to us smooth things.” He says Scripture is given for righteousness and good works, but people don’t want to hear what is right.

Short transcript

Do we only want to hear smooth things? Isaiah says, “Prophesy not unto us right things, speak unto us smooth things, prophesy deceits.” Pastor Kincer says they pay ministers to preach what they want to hear. They want to be told they are okay, but Scripture is given for righteousness.
