Is church really boring?

💡 Short answerIt can feel that way. Pastor Kincer says church was boring to him as a teenager because he wasn’t involved and had lost his desire for Jesus.

🔎 WhyPastor Kincer contrasts that with David, who was glad when they said, “Let us go into the house of the LORD.” Pastor Kincer says the church is made up of people, and the people determine what a church is.

Short transcript

Young people say church is boring. Pastor Kincer says he said the same thing at 16, 17 and 18, because he didn’t get involved and was a clock watcher. David said, “I was glad when they said unto me, Let us go into the house of the LORD.” Many Christians have lost that gladness and blame the church, but the church is made up of people.
