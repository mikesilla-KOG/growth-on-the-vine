Is God looking for fruit — or fireworks?

💡 Short answerFruit comes first. Love is the first fruit of the Spirit and the peak of God’s character, and you can’t have the manifestations of the Spirit without it.

🔎 WhyPastor Kincer notes that love is listed first among the fruit of the Spirit, that God is love, and that love sits at the top of sharing in the divine nature. His point: the fruit and character of God come before the manifestations.

Our short summary of this clip — not the preacher’s exact words.

🎙From the sermon

Pastor David G. Kincer · “A More Excellent Way” — his words

“Love, joy, peace. It’s the first fruit. 1 John 4:8 says, “God is love.” See, that is exactly what God is. He just gave us the description of God.”

“So the love is at the peak of divine character and it’s at the beginning of the fruits of the spirit. And believe me, Paul is telling us, you can’t have the manifestations of the spirit without the fruit and the divine character of God. That’s what he’s telling us. This is the way to get it.”

More on this clip

Paul isn’t anti-gifts. He’s saying you don’t get lasting spiritual manifestations by chasing them — you get them by becoming like God.

Love is the first fruit of the Spirit and the peak of divine character (2 Peter 1). Character first. Then the fireworks.

Ask yourself: Am I chasing manifestations — or becoming love?
