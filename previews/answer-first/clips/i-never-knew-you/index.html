Can I serve God and still miss knowing Him?

💡 Short answerYes. You can be used by God — even prophesy and do mighty works in Jesus’ name — and still miss the most important thing: love.

🔎 WhyPastor Kincer points to Jesus’ warning about people who prophesied and cast out demons in His name, yet hear “I never knew you.” He says they were used by God but missed love, which he describes as obeying God.

More on this clip

In A More Excellent Way, Pastor David G. Kincer walks from 1 Corinthians 13:2 into Jesus’ warning in Matthew 7: prophecy, mysteries, mountain-moving faith — without love — equal zero.

You can be used of God and still miss the important thing. Love — obedience-shaped relationship — is what was missing.

Ask yourself: Am I being used — or am I known?
