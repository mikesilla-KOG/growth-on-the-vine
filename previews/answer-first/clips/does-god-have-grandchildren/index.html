Does God have grandchildren?

💡 Short answerNo. God has no grandchildren and no stepchildren — only children.

🔎 WhyPastor Kincer says you have to be born again yourself and have your own first contact with Him. You can’t use your parents, your church or your minister as an excuse on judgment day.

Our short summary of this clip — not the preacher’s exact words.

🎙From the sermon

Pastor David G. Kincer · “Division Part 2” — his words

“The problem in America is if my granddad was a Baptist and my dad was a Baptist and I’m a Baptist, don’t bug me.”

▶ 13:19

“God does not have grandchildren. He does not have stepchildren. He has children. In other words, you got to get born again by him. You got to have that initial first contact with him.”

▶ 15:02

“I can’t use my parents for an excuse on judgment day. I can’t use my church or the minister on judgment day because I’m accountable to find him and serve him and follow him.”

▶ 15:27
