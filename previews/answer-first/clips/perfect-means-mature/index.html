Does “be perfect” mean never fail?

💡 Short answerNo. Pastor Kincer says “be perfect” means spiritual maturity and completeness, not a life with no flaws.

🔎 WhyPastor Kincer says people hear “be perfect” and think it means never sinning. He points to Hebrews 5:14 and James 1:4 to show it is about growing up and being complete, lacking nothing.

More on this clip

Jesus said, “Therefore you shall be perfect.” A lot of people hear that and shut down — “You can’t be perfect; we all sin.” They thought perfect meant a life with no flaws.

No — it means spiritual maturity, spiritual completeness. James 1:4: let patience have its perfect work, that you may be perfect and complete, lacking nothing. God is taking His people somewhere.

Ask yourself: Am I growing up — or staying a spiritual child?
