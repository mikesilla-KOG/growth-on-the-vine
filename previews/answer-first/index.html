  1. What if my gifts are just noise?A More Excellent Way

    Without love, even speaking in the languages of men and angels is just noise — a clanging cymbal.

    📖 1 Corinthians 13:1; 1 Corinthians 12:31

  2. Can I serve God and still miss knowing Him?A More Excellent Way

    Yes. You can be used by God — even prophesy and do mighty works in Jesus’ name — and still miss the most important thing: love.

    📖 Matthew 7:22–23; 1 Corinthians 13:2

  3. Does real love insist on its own way?A More Excellent Way

    No. Real love doesn’t demand its own way. It wants the right way — the truth.

    📖 1 Corinthians 13:4–5

  4. Is God looking for fruit — or fireworks?A More Excellent Way

    Fruit comes first. Love is the first fruit of the Spirit and the peak of God’s character, and you can’t have the manifestations of the Spirit without it.

    📖 Galatians 5:22–23; 1 John 4:8

  5. Does “be perfect” mean never fail?A More Excellent Way

    No. Pastor Kincer says “be perfect” means spiritual maturity and completeness, not a life with no flaws.

    📖 Matthew 5:48; James 1:4; Hebrews 5:14

  6. What is the real goal of God’s Word?A More Excellent Way

    Love from a pure heart. Paul says that is the goal of the commandment.

    📖 1 Timothy 1:5

  7. Do we want God’s blessings — or His rule?Saving a Nation

    God wants to rule our lives, not just bless them. Too many want heaven and His blessings without doing what He says.

    📖 1 Samuel 8:7; Luke 19:14

  8. Do we only want to hear smooth things?Saving a Nation

    Many people do. They want to be told they’re okay, so they hire ministers to say what they want to hear instead of what is right.

    📖 Isaiah 30:10; 2 Timothy 3:16

  9. What if a nation turns back to God?Saving a Nation

    God says He will relent from the judgment He planned. Pastor Kincer adds that Bible morality is an umbrella of protection when God judges.

    📖 Jeremiah 18:8; Jeremiah 12:17

  10. Can the Word still turn a nation?Saving a Nation

    Yes. Pastor Kincer says that if the church gets the message out, the Word does the work — it convicts and convinces people.

    📖 Isaiah 55:11

  11. Is church really boring?Division Part 1

    It can feel that way. Pastor Kincer says church was boring to him as a teenager because he wasn’t involved and had lost his desire for Jesus.

    📖 Psalm 122:1

  12. Should church be as consistent as a Big Mac?Division Part 1

    In what is preached, yes. Pastor Kincer says the Holy Ghost, salvation, righteousness and holy living should be preached the same in every church.

    📖 1 Corinthians 1:10

  13. Can a divided home still stand?Division Part 1

    No. Jesus said a house divided against itself will not stand.

    📖 Matthew 12:25

  14. Who was really crucified for you?Division Part 1

    Christ was — not Paul, and not any church name, group or minister.

    📖 1 Corinthians 1:12–13

  15. Does God have grandchildren?Division Part 2

    No. God has no grandchildren and no stepchildren — only children.

    📖 John 3:3
