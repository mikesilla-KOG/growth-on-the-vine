🎙From the sermonThe Blessing— September 12, 2021 · Michael Silla▶ 0:00

A younger speaker teaches that God’s blessings of fruit, peace and protection in Leviticus 26 come with a condition: if you walk in His statutes and keep His commandments. Abimelech, Zechariah and Elizabeth, and Daniel show the blessing; Judas and Ananias show the curse. He urges fighting for God’s law in the nation, tells Walt Heyer’s story, and closes with 2 Chronicles 7:14 and communion.
