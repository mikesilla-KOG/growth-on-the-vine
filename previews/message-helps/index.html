Where does the ministry of helps fit among the gifts?

▶ Watch this part (0:00)

He ranks the utterance ministries (apostle, prophet, teacher), then sign ministries (miracles, healings), then work ministries anyone can do—helps and governments—with tongues last. Paul the apostle still helped set up and clean up; good leaders work with the crew.
