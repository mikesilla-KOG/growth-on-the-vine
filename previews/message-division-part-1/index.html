Division Part 1

The Corinthians series · Part 1 · Pastor David G. Kincer, Vine Cluster Church · Uploaded June 18, 2014 · 22:44

Watch the full message on YouTube

Summary

If you have ever wondered why so many young people drift away from church, or whether church is just boring, this message takes those questions head-on. Pastor David G. Kincer responds to a new book that blames the church for young people leaving. He says the complaints are not new. When he stayed away as a teenager it was his own decision, and church felt boring because he never got involved. His challenge is to stop blaming and take responsibility: get in and make a difference.

He then turns to the opening of 1 Corinthians, where he points out that the first thing the apostle Paul corrects in a “very good church” is division. Using the Big Mac, which tastes the same in every country, he says the gospel, salvation and holy living should be taught the same way in every church. Citing Jesus’ words in Matthew 12:25, he says a divided kingdom, city or home will not stand, and he urges husbands and wives to stand together in the same truth so their children can see Jesus in them.

Finally he looks at the four groups in Corinth: “I am of Paul,” “of Apollos,” “of Peter,” “of Christ.” Only one is right, he says. Our unity is found in Jesus and the Scriptures, not in a minister, a denomination or whoever baptized us. He closes by saying new believers and older believers can be fed at the same table, so whether you are brand new or have been around for years, there is a place for you.

How the message flows

Each point links to that moment in the video.

  1. A new book blames the church

    He responds to a book saying the church caused young people to leave. He says the 75% statistic is not new, and when he stayed away it was his own decision, not the church’s fault.

    ▶ 0:00

  2. Take responsibility and make a difference

    God told him not to complain about the church but to get in and make a difference. The Spirit gives power to make wise decisions, and it is easier to blame a church than to take responsibility.

    ▶ 1:31

  3. “The church doesn’t embrace science”

    He calls that claim a lie: there is more material on science and the Bible today than ever, so no young person has an excuse for not understanding.

    ▶ 2:53

  4. “Church is boring”

    He said the same at 16 to 18, because he would not get involved. David was glad to go to the house of the Lord (Psalm 122:1), and the church is made up of people.

    ▶ 3:42

  5. “The church is intolerant”

    He says the church cannot change the Scriptures to fit public policy; it must preach what the Bible says, and everyone answers to God for their choices.

    ▶ 5:56

  6. Why this series, and why the suit

    He explains why he is wearing a suit for two months and where the focus has been lost. Let God be true and every man a liar: we line up with the Scriptures, not the other way around.

    ▶ 6:53

  7. Paul’s first confrontation: division

    Starting, developing and governing a New Testament church begins with unity. Paul’s first correction to the Corinthians is “speak the same thing” (1 Corinthians 1:10).

    ▶ 8:45

  8. The Big Mac: speak the same thing

    A Big Mac is identical in Salvador, Brazil, Korea, China and every state. In the same way the Holy Ghost, salvation and holy living should be preached the same in every church.

    ▶ 9:26

  9. Why unity matters: a divided house will not stand

    Why did Paul raise division first? Jesus said a kingdom, city or house divided against itself will not stand (Matthew 12:25), and a divided people loses the war.

    ▶ 11:31

  10. Unity in the home

    Husband and wife need the same truth and the same moral standards, and children need to see Jesus lived out, not just talked about. Divided homes are why kids want out.

    ▶ 12:44

  11. Same mind, right judgment, no quarreling

    Be joined in the same mind and judgment, judge by the Word of God rather than personal opinion (John 7:24), and stop airing church disagreements to the world.

    ▶ 14:39

  12. Four groups, one right: “I am of Christ”

    Paul, Apollos, Peter or Christ? Only the Christ group is right. Our unity is in Christ, who is one with the Father and the Scriptures, not in a church’s opinions or our own.

    ▶ 15:39

  13. Was Paul crucified for you?

    No pope, priest, denomination or famous minister can save anyone. Our focus is Christ and the Scriptures (1 Corinthians 1:13).

    ▶ 16:51

  14. Not every road leads to the same God

    He says he will say more about Mormons and Muslims later in the message. For now, he says that re-baptizing converts, denying that Christ died on the cross, and denying that Jesus is God do not fit the Bible, so not every road leads to the same God.

    ▶ 17:49

  15. Three statements from Paul

    Follow and imitate Jesus, not a minister or church group. Focus on Jesus, not on who baptized you. Preach Christ, his gospel and the cross, where the power of God is (1 Corinthians 1:14-18).

    ▶ 19:53

  16. New and older believers at one table

    He answers the claim that new and older believers cannot share a meeting: at one dinner table you can serve milk and meat together, so we can have it all in one setting.

    ▶ 21:49

I didn’t want any church or any God bridling me and telling me what to do. Don’t tell me what I can and can’t do.
