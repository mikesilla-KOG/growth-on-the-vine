🎙From the sermonDoes One's Faith Justify One's Immorality? · Pastor David G. Kincer▶ 0:00

Pastor David G. Kincer asks whether any faith can justify immorality that hurts self or others. After defining faith from the dictionary and Hebrews 11 / Romans 4 / John 8 / James 2, he surveys humanist, evolutionist, homosexual, liberal and religionist groups that treat some harm as moral if consensual, then details humanism’s requirements (reject Jesus, accept evolution-based morals) and consequences (STDs, abuse, forced compliance). He concludes one’s faith does not justify harmful lifestyles and challenges humanists to join Bible morality and Americans to vote Bible-morality candidates—not party labels.
