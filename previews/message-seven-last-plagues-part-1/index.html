What are the seven last plagues in Revelation 15:1, and have they happened?

▶ Watch this part (0:01)

Short answer: He says the seven last plagues “concludes the wrath of God,” and that there is “no historical Bible evidence” that they have literally taken place. His conclusion is that something will happen before Jesus returns that gets the world’s attention and leaves people “a choice to repent and serve Jesus Christ or not repent.”

🎙From the sermonTranscript of this part · Pastor David G. Kincer

▶ 0:01 Today we’re going to be talking about what the Bible calls the seven last plagues in the book of Revelation. That concludes the wrath of God; it finishes it, brings it to an end. It says in Revelation 15:1, “I saw another sign in heaven, great and marvelous: seven angels having the seven last plagues, for in them the wrath of God is complete.” So these last seven are the end of these things. Now, here’s a little picture of the seven. There is no historical Bible evidence that any of these last plagues have literally taken place, so that only leaves one conclusion: there’s something going to happen before Jesus returns that will get the world’s population’s attention and leave them with a choice to repent and serve Jesus Christ or not repent. So something very influential is going to be happening in these last days to separate the good from the bad and give people a chance to accept Christ.

What do the first four bowls of wrath do? (Revelation 16:2-9)

▶ Watch this part (1:31)

Short answer: He walks through the first four bowls: painful sores on those with the mark of the beast, the sea becoming blood, the fresh water turned to blood “for the martyrs,” and scorching heat, after which people still “blasphemed God.” He says he is not sure how humans live in the sea and that “it could be an allegory.”

▶ 1:31 So the first angel poured out his bowl upon the earth, and there were painful sores on the people with the mark of the beast and those that worship the beast. In the book of Revelation, have you seen it? It didn’t pay to worship the beast; it didn’t pay to get his mark. They’re always being tormented; something’s always bad happening to them. The second angel poured out his bowl, and the sea became dead men’s blood, and all living souls in the sea die. Now, I don’t know how humans live in the sea, unless somewhere in the future we build cities underneath the ocean, but it could be on boats, could be those beside the sea, the oceans, and it could be an allegory. But anyway, the only place where men are dying in these seven last plagues is here in the sea, or things are dying; could be just the creatures of the sea.

▶ 2:44 Then he turns all the fresh water to blood for the martyrs. You remember back in the seals, there had to be a number of martyrs, people that had died for Jesus’ name, for Jesus’ way, for his book, for his truth. They had to be a certain number that were martyred, killed because they loved Jesus and served Jesus, before Jesus would return. And here we find it again in these last plagues: he gives these people that murder God’s people the blood to drink, and the angel said, “This is just, because they shed your people’s blood, and you’ve given them blood to drink.” Then the fourth angel poured out his bowl, and he had power to scorch people on earth with severe heat, and they blasphemed God; they spoke evil of God and they blasphemed God’s name.
