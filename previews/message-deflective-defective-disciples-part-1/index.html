Why did many of Jesus’ disciples leave him?

▶ Watch this part (0:00)

Short answer: Pastor Kincer says these were disciples who did not believe: they were disciplined to follow Christ to a certain degree, but they left when it came down to God being involved, and to accountability and responsibility. He says Jesus told them you cannot decide to serve God on your own, since the Father and the Spirit must be involved in your salvation, and it is not about joining a church or being a Jew, Protestant or Catholic.

🎙From the sermonTranscript of this part · Pastor David G. Kincer

▶ 0:00 But this morning we’re going to talk about some deflective, defective disciples. Not everybody in the Bible turned out to be heroes. Deflective, defective disciples. They deflected because they had a defect. A disciple is one that’s disciplined, a pupil of Christ, a follower of Christ. And in John 6 we find him talking to what he called disciples, and he said, “There are some of you that believe not.” These are disciples that believe not. “For Jesus knew from the beginning who believe not and who should betray him.” Wow, powerful statement. “No man,” Jesus said, “can come unto me except it were given unto him by my Father.”

▶ 1:03 Jesus said, listen, you can’t decide to serve God on your own. Me and my Father have to be involved in your conversion, in your salvation. It’s not joining a church, or being a Jew, or being a Protestant, or being a Catholic. Jesus said my Father has to be involved. The Spirit of God has to be involved. That’s why the Bible says you must be born again of the Spirit and of the word. Well, the disciples didn’t like this statement. Okay, now these are disciples. They’re disciples that didn’t believe. See, they were disciplined to follow Christ to a certain degree, but “from that time many of his disciples went back and walked no more with him.” They left him. And they were called disciples, but when it come down to God being involved, and accountability and responsibility, they left Jesus. And we’re going to show you what was really in their heart here.
