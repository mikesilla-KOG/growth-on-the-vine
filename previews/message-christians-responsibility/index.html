What does it mean that Christians are the light of the world? (Matthew 5:14-16)

▶ Watch this part (0:00)

Short answer: He reads Matthew 5:14-16 and asks, “why are Christians hiding out?” He says our responsibility is to let our light shine before men, which means that they “may see your good works.”

▶ 0:00 Uh, we’re going to be talking about Christians’ responsibility. They hide out in their homes, they hide out in the church. They’re worthless. “Nor do they light a lamp and put it under a bushel. A city set on a hill cannot be hid. ▶ Watch this part (2:00)
