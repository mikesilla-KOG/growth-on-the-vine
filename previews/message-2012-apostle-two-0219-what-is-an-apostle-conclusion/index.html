🎙From the sermon2012 Apostle Two 0219: What Is an Apostle? (Conclusion) · Pastor David G. Kincer▶ 0:00

In this concluding message on “What Is an Apostle?” Pastor David G. Kincer teaches from 1 Corinthians 9:18–27. He says an apostle is a delegated leader under Jesus Christ who makes himself a servant to win people, becomes “all things to all men” without joining their sin, and runs the race with self-control and confidence so that he is not disqualified. He explains the shadow laws and says that salvation requires keeping the qualifications to the end.
