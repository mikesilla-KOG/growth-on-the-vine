What does it mean that the seven stars are in his right hand? (Revelation 2:1, Romans 10:14–15, Matthew 10:1)

▶ Watch this part (0:00)

Short answer: He says “the seven stars are the seven messengers of the seven churches of Asia,” and that all of us must be in the Lord’s hands, “not in a denomination’s hands, not in a religious group, not our own opinions and our own ideas, not self-appointed.” He cites Romans 10:14–15, that “no man takes the priesthood upon himself, he has to be selected by God,” and Matthew 10:1, where Jesus “called unto him his 12 disciples.”
