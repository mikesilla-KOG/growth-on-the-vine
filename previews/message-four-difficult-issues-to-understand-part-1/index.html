In this message Pastor David G. Kincer begins a look at four difficult issues in Revelation 20: the bottomless pit, Satan bound and loosed, the thousand years, and the first resurrection. He first questions the pre-tribulation rapture picture, then shows from Luke, Romans and Revelation what the bottomless pit is. He argues that Satan has been bound and loosed before, citing 1 Chronicles 21, Job and Luke 10, and that Satan was cast out of the earth when Jesus was lifted up.
