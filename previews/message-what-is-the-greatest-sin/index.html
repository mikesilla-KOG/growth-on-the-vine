What is the greatest sin?

▶ Watch this part (0:08)

Short answer: Sin is the transgression of the law, so breaking the greatest commandment—to love God with all your heart, soul and mind—is the greatest sin.

The greatest sin. The Bible says in first John sin is the transgression of the law so if sin is the transgression of the law then breaking the greatest law would be the greatest sin. And in Matthew 22:37 Jesus tells us what the greatest sin is you shall love the Lord your God with all your heart with all your soul with all your mind. This is the first and great commandment.
