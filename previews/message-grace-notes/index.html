▶ Watch this part (22:56)

Short answer: He says “you may not be an apostle, but you can still be a light to the world. Are you increasing in grace, or is your grace limited?

▶ Watch this part (24:52)

Short answer: He says some Christians “just take favor, go to heaven, wait for the rapture,” while others “grow in grace” and so have more influence in “changing people’s lives and bringing them to Christ.” He asks, “are you increasing in grace, or is your grace limited?” and says grace “will change that and put a focus on Jesus and his focus on others.”

🎙From the sermonTranscript of this part · Pastor David G. Kincer

▶ 24:52 See, why is it that some Christians are concerned about others, are concerned about, about going to church, and are concerned about growing? They’ve entered into a dimension of grace. See, that’s influencing them. Others, they just take favor, go to heaven, wait for the rapture, that’s it. They do what they want, they stay home, they don’t pray, they don’t, you know, they can’t win souls to Christ because they don’t know the word or anything else. But others, they respond to that grace, and they grow in grace, and the more they grow, the more influence they have in helping people and changing people’s lives and bringing them to Christ. My question, or the, I should say the Holy Spirit’s question to us this morning is: are you increasing in grace, or is your grace limited? If you don’t like things or you, you don’t want to do things, you just don’t do them. You got no influence of God to overcome your personal desires and wishes and laziness and attitude and beliefs. Everything’s got to be for you and focused on you. Grace will change that and put a focus on Jesus and his focus on others to come to him and grow in him.
