Does faith let us live however we want?

▶ Watch this part (0:00)

Short answer: Romans 1:17: the just shall live by faith—but Romans 2:6 says God will render to every man according to his deeds. Good works don’t save us, yet Scripture says again and again we are judged by our works (Psalm 62:12; Jeremiah 17:10; 32:19), and our light should shine with good works.
