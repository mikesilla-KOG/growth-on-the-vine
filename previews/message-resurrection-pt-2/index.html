Why is the sting of death sin?

▶ Watch this part (0:00)

Short answer: He says the sting of death is sin because the wages of sin is death, and James 1:15 shows that lust gives birth to sin and sin brings forth death. Sin starts with lust, he says, but the desire “doesn’t mean a hill of beans” until you act upon it, and that is when you violate God’s word and death sets in.

🎙From the sermonTranscript of this part · Pastor David G. Kincer

▶ 0:00 “Death, where is your sting? Death, where is your victory?” Where is your conquest? Quoting out of Scripture again. “The sting of death is sin, and the strength of sin is the law.” Why is the sting of death sin? Well, Romans 6:23, “The wages of sin is” what? Death. You see, when lust has conceived, in James 1:15, it gives birth to sin, and sin, when it is full grown, brings forth death. See, so sin starts with lust, but that lust does not mean anything till you act upon it. See, I can desire a woman outside my marriage, but it doesn’t mean a hill of beans until I what? Act upon it. That’s when I violate God’s word, and that’s when death sets in. I can want to kill somebody, “Oh, they got me so upset.” See, my desire, but if I don’t activate it, it doesn’t produce sin. Are you there?

Is having a bad thought the same as sinning?

▶ Watch this part (1:23)

Short answer: He says no: “the thought isn’t the sin, the wrong desire isn’t the sin; the sin is when you activate that wrong desire.” If you are not activating the thought, he says, don’t beat yourself up. He then says there is another death everyone must confront, the lake of fire, by our deeds and how we treated other people.
