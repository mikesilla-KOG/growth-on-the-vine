In “Jesus’ Processing Plan, Part 1,” Pastor David G. Kincer walks through Mark 3 to Mark 6 to show how Jesus called, made and sent his disciples: they followed him, responded to his call, spent time with him, were taught and shown his authority, and only then were sent to preach repentance, heal and cast out devils. He says everything starts with following Jesus and that authority comes only from Christ, not from titles or prophecies.
