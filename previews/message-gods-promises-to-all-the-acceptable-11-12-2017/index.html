Pastor David G. Kincer teaches that God has promises for “the acceptable”—those like Cornelius who fear God and do right by conscience: the Spirit poured on all flesh, the earth filled with his glory, transformation into Christ’s image, knowing God, and wisdom from above. From Acts 19 he asks whether believers have received the Holy Ghost, urges adding virtue to faith, and teaches that any believer can lay on hands.
