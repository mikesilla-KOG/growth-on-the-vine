In this message Pastor David G. Kincer continues his series on stages of Christian growth and explains the child stage: those who are born again and now obey the Word. What are the embryo and newborn stages of growth in the Christian life?

▶ Watch this part (0:00)

Short answer: He says in the embryo stage people respond to hearing the Word: some reject or mock it and say it is “an inconvenient time,” while others believe, repent and follow Christ, “not a church, not an author, not an apostle.” In the newborn stage “their sins are forgiven” and they “desire the sincere milk of the Word.”

▶ 0:00 So today we’re going to look at the child stage of growth from the Bible. Okay, remember the embryo stage is when they hear the Word. It’s how they responded when they heard the Word. And they rejected the Word, they mocked when they heard the Word and rejected the Word. They not only mocked, but some of them abused the Bible, Jesus, and His followers, mistreated, lambasted, put down, persecuted others. Said, “It’s not a convenient time, it’s an inconvenient time. Not a church, not an author, not an apostle, but they followed Christ.

▶ 1:14 The newborn stage, you remember, their sins are forgiven. They come to Jesus to learn from Him.
