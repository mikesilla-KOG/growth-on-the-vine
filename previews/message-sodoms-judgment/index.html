What does God promise in the midst of judgment?

▶ Watch this part (0:00)

Short answer: He says he will look at “precious promises before, during, and after Sodom’s judgment,” and that in the midst of war, famine and captivity God has another message for people who will listen to the message of repentance. He says Jeremiah spoke shortly before the captivity while nations warred around Judah and false prophets said nothing would happen, and asks, “Does it sound like America today?”
