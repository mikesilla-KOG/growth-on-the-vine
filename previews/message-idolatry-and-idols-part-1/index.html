In this message Pastor David G. Kincer says America has its own idols in entertainment and compares them with the gods of ancient Corinth. He walks through 1 Corinthians 8, where Paul answers those who rely on human knowledge about food offered to idols, and explains the weak conscience and the stumbling block. He then connects idolatry to spiritual adultery and to 1 Corinthians 10, where Paul says to flee from idolatry and uses communion as an illustration.
