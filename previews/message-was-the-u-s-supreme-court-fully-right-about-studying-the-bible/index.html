What did the 1980 Supreme Court say about studying the Bible?

▶ Watch this part (0:00)

Short answer: He says the 1980 decision, quoting 1963—two witnesses—declared the Bible may constitutionally be used in appropriate study of history, civilization, ethics (Bible morality), comparative religions, or the like.

🎙From the sermonTranscript of this part · Pastor David G. Kincer

▶ 0:00 Today we’re going to ask this question: was the United States Supreme Court fully right about the constitutional right to study the Bible? We’re going to remind you of the 1980 Supreme Court decision that quoted from a 1963 Supreme Court decision—so thus two witnesses confirming. And this is what the Supreme Court said: the Bible may constitutionally be used in an appropriate study of history, civilization, ethics—what we know as morality, Bible morality—comparative religions, or the like. The Supreme Court thusly said it is constitutionally legal.
