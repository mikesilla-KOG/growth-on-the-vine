Founding Pastor David G. Kincer

Pastor David G. Kincer is the founding pastor of Vine Cluster Church, and many of the messages on this site come from his teaching. In his own words, he has been a follower of Christ for more than sixty years and a pastor for more than fifty, preaching in streets, prisons, churches, and third-world nations.

He has carried the Bible well beyond Lake Elsinore, teaching it in Central, South and North America, Europe, Africa, China, and South Korea. He is still teaching today: new Bible studies and messages from Pastor Kincer continue to be shared on the Vine Cluster YouTube channel, and he spoke as a guest at the church in July 2026.

Our story

Vine Cluster Church began in Orange County in the late 1960s, reaching out to hippies and street people with food, music, a place to rest, medical help, Christian counseling, and the truth of the Bible. Many people left lives of drugs and hardship and became followers of Jesus. In 2005 the church moved to Lake Elsinore, where it continues today, with people of every age, background, and walk of life.

The church’s welcome points to two Scriptures: Jesus is the vine, and His people are the branches who bear fruit when they stay close to Him (John 15:5); and the new wine is found in the cluster, where a blessing is (Isaiah 65:8). That’s what we hope to help you do: believe, know, and grow.
