🎙From the sermonA Quick Change of Government 11/26/2017 · Unnamed speaker (not Pastor Kincer)▶ 0:00

This message tells how King Hezekiah inherited a nation ruined by his father Ahaz and turned it around quickly: he reopened the temple doors, cleansed out the filth, restored priests, offerings and David’s pattern of worship, and called all Israel to Passover. Despite mockery, a few humbled themselves, idols came down, tithing returned, and God delivered Judah from Assyria.
