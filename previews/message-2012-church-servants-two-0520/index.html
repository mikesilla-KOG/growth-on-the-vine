In this message Pastor David G. Kincer continues through 1 Corinthians 16 with the devoted leaders, the ones who serve behind the scenes and refresh others. He teaches that it matters who you follow, that unity and music in worship are tied to God’s presence, and that “Anathema” in verse 22 is about not loving the Lord Jesus Christ. He closes by saying that loving Jesus means keeping his commandments and learning how to treat other people, and that making Jesus Lord of your life keeps false leaders from leading you astray.
