After worship and prayer for the Spirit, the message contrasts Saul and David. Saul was given a new heart but chose to please people, lost God’s presence and turned on David; David, despite his sin, repented and fought for God with his heart, his mouth and his love. The church must love God first, renew its heart and become a David church.
