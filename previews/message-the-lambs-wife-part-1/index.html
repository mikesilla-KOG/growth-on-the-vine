Can anyone predict when Jesus returns, and who is the “Lamb’s Wife”?

▶ Watch this part (0:00)

Short answer: He says the pre-tribulation rapture theory “is not the most widely believed doctrine of the second coming” and that we should study “like the Bereans.” He says the Lamb’s Wife “is the New Jerusalem and is the church,” and that both date-setting and “teaser predictions” are “very destructive” because “nobody knows.”
