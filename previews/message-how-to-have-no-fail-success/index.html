In “How to Have No Fail Success,” Pastor David G. Kincer walks through the new covenant promises in Ezekiel 11, 36 and 37 — one heart, a new spirit, a heart of flesh, cleansing by the word, and the covenant of peace that makes believers priests — and shows where the New Testament says the same. He teaches that God says “I will” and does the changing, while we must respond, seek Him and apply ourselves. He closes with accounts of early martyrs and the deathbed words of well-known unbelievers.
