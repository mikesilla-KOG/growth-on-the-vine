What does being a mother require?

▶ Watch this part (0:01)

Short answer: A mother cares for her child’s health, needs and education—and most importantly influences what goes into the child’s mind. Ephesians 6:4: bring them up in the nurture and admonition of the Lord, knowing God holds you accountable. Proverbs 22:6: train up a child in the way he should go, and when he is old he will not depart from it.
