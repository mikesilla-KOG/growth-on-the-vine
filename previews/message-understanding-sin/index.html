Why did God reject Cain’s offering if produce was acceptable?

▶ Watch this part (0:00)

Short answer: Genesis 4: Cain’s produce offering vs Abel’s flock; God respected Abel and his offering, not Cain. Leviticus 5:7–11 shows flour from the ground can be a sin offering, so rejection was not because fruit of the ground was invalid. Shadow offerings never took away sin (Hebrews 10:4); they pointed to Jesus the Lamb. Isaiah 1: God wants people to stop evil and learn to do good.
