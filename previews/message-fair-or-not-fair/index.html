In this message Pastor David G. Kincer walks through Ezekiel 18, the “laws of life and death,” and answers the charge that “the way of the Lord is not fair.” He teaches that each person is judged by his own ways, that God takes no pleasure in the death of the wicked, and that God offers a new heart, mind and spirit. He then turns to Ezekiel 33 and the watchman, and calls the church to speak what God said to speak.
