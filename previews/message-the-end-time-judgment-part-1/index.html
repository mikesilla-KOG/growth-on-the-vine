What are the three revelations in the book of Revelation, and why can’t John add to the word?

▶ Watch this part (0:00)

Short answer: He says there are three revelations in Revelation: the revelation of Jesus Christ, of His church, and of the judgment of God, “the same revelation that’s in the rest of the New Testament and the rest of the Old Testament.” He says John could not add to or take away from the word, so if something is not found in the rest of the scriptures “we’re missing the revelation of revelations.”
