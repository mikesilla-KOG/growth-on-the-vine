In this short message Pastor David G. Kincer teaches that God judges and punishes His people when they behave worse than Sodom. He uses Jude 1:7 and Luke 17:28 to show Sodom as an example and argues that the Bible, not science, tells us what is an allegory. He then begins Ezekiel 16, where God lists abominations His people commit, starting with trusting in your own beauty and playing the harlot because of your fame.
