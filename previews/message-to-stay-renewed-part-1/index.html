🎙From the sermonTo Stay Renewed Part 1 · Pastor David G. Kincer▶ 0:00

In this message Pastor David G. Kincer teaches that it is as important to stay saved as to get saved, and gives the Bible’s procedure for renewed lives to stay renewed. Using 1 Corinthians 6, Hebrews 6:12 and Hebrews 12:1-2, he says to stop being lazy, be around people who possess the promises, lay aside every hindrance, run with patience, and look to Jesus. Along the way he compares Jesus to government and speaks on politics, culture and history.
