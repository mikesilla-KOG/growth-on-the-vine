Ask a question

Ask a Bible question and see an answer built from two things, kept clearly apart: what Scripture says (NKJV), and what Pastor David G. Kincer teaches in our sermons, with a link to the exact moment in the video. If no sermon covers your question, we say so and answer from Scripture only.

This is a demo with sample answers. The answers below were prepared ahead of time from real NKJV text and the real sermon transcripts. The question box is not switched on yet, so typing a question will not produce a new answer.

Where Pastor Kincer looks at a question from more than one side, we show both. Where he has not spoken to it in these messages, we tell you plainly instead of guessing.

Live answers need a small private service that is not set up yet. For now, try one of the sample questions below.
