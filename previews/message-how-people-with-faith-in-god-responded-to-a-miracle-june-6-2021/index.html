🎙From the sermonHow People with Faith in God Responded to a Miracle — June 6, 2021 · Pastor David G. Kincer▶ 0:00

Pastor David G. Kincer walks through John 9 and how people who believed in God responded when Jesus opened the eyes of a man born blind. Nobody had faith for the healing; the man believed after Jesus spoke to him, testified about what he experienced, and was cast out. He says faith is overemphasized, tells how he was saved at an altar in Norwalk, California, and calls the church to invite Jesus through praise, assembling, prayer and the word.
