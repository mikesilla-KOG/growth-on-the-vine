Walking through Stephen’s sermon in Acts 7, this message shows how Moses was a prophet like Jesus: both fled a king, were rejected by their own, were sent by God to deliver, and did signs and wonders. The Red Sea pictures the torn veil, the golden calf pictures man-made religion, and God wants to dwell not in buildings but in us by his Spirit.
