Can spiritual powers separate a born-again believer from God’s love? (Romans 8:38-39)

▶ Watch this part (0:02)

Short answer: He says no: when you are born again and really committed to Christ, “you’re not going to backslide.” You might “fall and you might fail and goof up,” but “that spirit that you’ve been born with is going to pick you back up,” which he calls “the difference between religion and reality.”

🎙From the sermonTranscript of this part · Pastor David G. Kincer

▶ 0:02 Romans 8:38: “I am persuaded that neither death nor life, nor angels nor principalities nor powers shall separate us from the love of God which is in Christ Jesus our Lord.” So is there spiritual powers? Are they trying to separate us? Can they? No. When you’re born again and you’re really committed to Christ, you’re not going to backslide. You might fall and you might fail and goof up and go through a cold season or a dumb time, but that Spirit that you’ve been born with is going to pick you back up. It’s going to convict you, it’s going to agitate you until you get back to being a son of the living God. See, that’s the difference between religion and reality. Some people are just thinking in their head they’re Christians, when it’s got to be a birthing of the heart.

Which plagues of Egypt appear in Revelation, and what power did Pharaoh’s sorcerers have?

▶ Watch this part (3:21)

Short answer: He says seven of Egypt’s ten plagues are used in Revelation, and that God’s people were still on earth during them rather than raptured. He says the sorcerers had only “limited supernatural power,” and “they can only do what God limits them to do,” so it “was working” against them.

🎙From the sermonTranscript of this part · Pastor David G. Kincer

▶ 3:21 Now let’s take a look at Egypt’s plagues here. Seven of the 10 plagues used in Egypt, when God went down to plague Egypt and get his people out, are used in Revelation: the water to blood, the locusts, the boils, the hail, the darkness, the death of the firstborn, the frogs. And the people of God were still where? On earth, weren’t they? They didn’t get raptured; they were still on earth. Most of them would not what? Repent. Isn’t that true, including Pharaoh? Just wouldn’t repent, after all these signs and all this. The sorcerers did signs. Remember, they weren’t doing anything till Moses came along, and then Moses came along changing a rod to a snake, and then they changed their rod to a snake. Never did that before. Why? Because God gave them limited power to do it at that time. They can only do what God limits them to do.

▶ 4:40 So they turn water to blood. Did they need more snakes? No, they needed to get rid of Moses’s snake. Did they need more blood? No, they needed to turn the water back to clear water, but they couldn’t. Why? ‘Cause God wouldn’t allow it. But he allowed them to change more water to blood; that isn’t what they needed. Are you there? And then they brought frogs up. Moses brought frogs up, and so the sorcerers, they brought frogs up. Did they need more frogs? No, they wanted to get rid of the frogs. Moses, I mean Pharaoh, asked them, “Get rid of the frogs,” Moses. So the sorcerers are getting more of what they want to get rid of, and even though they had limited supernatural powers, it was working what? Against them, and it was obvious that they had no power at all.
