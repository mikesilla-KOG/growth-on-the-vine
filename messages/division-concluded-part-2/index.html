Division Concluded Part 2: full transcript and answers

The Corinthians series · Division Concluded, Part 2 · Pastor David G. Kincer, Vine Cluster Church · Uploaded June 18, 2014 · 22:05

Watch the full message on YouTube

In “Division Concluded Part 2,” Pastor David G. Kincer finishes the Corinthians series on division with 1 Corinthians 4:17-21. He uses the Marines to show that unity can be done, says we cannot pick and choose in church, and says the kingdom of God is in power, not words. He says Christians should unite around Jesus, the gospel and the cross rather than argue over details, and he closes by asking how we can tell a real minister, and what each member can contribute.

Can you pick and choose in church?

▶ Watch this part (3:30)

Short answer: He asks whether a Marine could show up late for reveille or skip calisthenics, and answers “No way.” In church, he says, “we show up when we want to,” and the church has been led to believe “we can pick and choose.” He says that is Paul’s argument: it is the Corinthians’ own prideful thinking, and it cripples those who follow them.
