Full Messages

Each sermon below is written out in full, organized by the questions it answers, with Scripture (NKJV) and a link to the exact moment in the video.

  • Saving a Nation

    June 18, 2014 · 32:52

    In “Saving a Nation,” Pastor David G. Kincer says the whole Bible, Old Testament included, was written for our learning, and uses Judges 17–18 (Micah, the tribe of Dan and the people of Laish) to picture a land where everyone does what is right in his own eyes. He says the church must stop living careless and self-confident, speak the Word of God in public, and unite over Bible morality. He also speaks about politics, entitlements, scientism and several controversial moral issues, and says the turning of a nation starts with the church.

  • Division Part 1

    June 18, 2014 · 22:44

    In “Division Part 1,” Pastor David G. Kincer answers a new book that blames the church for young people leaving, saying the complaints are not new and the answer is to stop blaming and get in and make a difference. He then turns to 1 Corinthians 1:10, where Paul’s first correction of the Corinthians is division, and says our unity is in Jesus and the Scriptures, not in a minister, a denomination or whoever baptized us. Along the way he speaks about church boredom, science, intolerance, the home, and other religions.

  • Division Part 2

    June 18, 2014 · 22:49

    In “Division Part 2,” Pastor David G. Kincer continues through 1 Corinthians 1–2 and says unity is found in the power of God and the Spirit, not in excellent speaking, intellect or a favorite minister. He tells of his own changed life at the altar, why he recommends the baptism of the Holy Ghost, and why God has no grandchildren. He says we need God every day and not only in an emergency, and that love is spelled T-I-M-E.

  • Division Concluded Part 1

    June 18, 2014 · 22:50

    In “Division Concluded Part 1,” Pastor David G. Kincer finishes the section of 1 Corinthians (chapter 4) where Paul deals with division. He contrasts how the Corinthians saw themselves with how real ministers lived, and lists Paul’s sufferings. Paul answers insults with good words, works with his own hands and asks for unity even when picked on. He says Paul wants fathers rather than mere talkers, and that the goal of the gospel is Christ formed in you.

  • Division Concluded Part 2

    June 18, 2014 · 22:05

    In “Division Concluded Part 2,” Pastor David G. Kincer finishes the Corinthians series on division with 1 Corinthians 4:17-21. He uses the Marines to show that unity can be done, says we cannot pick and choose in church, and says the kingdom of God is in power, not words. He says Christians should unite around Jesus, the gospel and the cross rather than argue over details, and he closes by asking how we can tell a real minister, and what each member can contribute.

  • A More Excellent Way

    June 18, 2014 (preached March 25, 2012) · 29:19

    In “A More Excellent Way,” preached March 25, 2012, Pastor David G. Kincer teaches from 1 Corinthians 12:31–13:13 that love is the more excellent way to the spiritual manifestations. Without love, he says, tongues, prophecy, knowledge, faith, giving and even martyrdom are worth nothing. He defines love from 1 Corinthians 13, says everyone in the church has a part and must grow up out of childish things, and says the goal of the commandment is love out of a pure heart.
