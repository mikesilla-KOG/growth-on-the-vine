How are the dead raised, and with what body do they come?

▶ Watch this part (0:00)

Short answer: He says Paul explains the resurrection of the dead in great detail starting at 1 Corinthians 15:35, and that Paul saw Jesus personally after He ascended. The two questions are honest ones from people with common sense, he says, like the Sadducees, who knew the body decayed, and Paul answers by calling them “fool,” which means stupid or without reason, and by arguing from common sense and then Scripture.

He says the seed germinates and comes alive on the inside first, and that is how we start, “on the inside first,” then comes the outward fruit, and every seed gets its own body.
