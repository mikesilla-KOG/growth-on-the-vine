What is Leviathan, and can anyone overcome him? (Isaiah 27:1, Job 41:1–10)

▶ Watch this part (0:00)

Short answer: He says Leviathan is a serpent: in Isaiah 27:1 the Lord “will punish Leviathan the fleeing serpent,” “the twisted serpent.” Turning to Job 41, he says “any hope of overcoming Leviathan is false” and “man cannot defeat this snake.” He says God hides mysteries by switching between the natural and the spiritual, and “only the Holy Ghost can reveal them to you.”
