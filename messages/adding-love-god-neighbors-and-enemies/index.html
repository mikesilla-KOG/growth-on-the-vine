🎙From the sermonAdding Love: God, Neighbors, and Enemies · Pastor David G. Kincer▶ 0:00

The speaker says love must be added to faith: as a noun God is love (1 John 4:8), and as a verb we love God, neighbor, and enemies with action (Matthew 22; 5:44). Loving God means keeping commandments; loving neighbor means doing no harm (Romans 13:8). Luke 6’s turn-the-cheek pattern is for non-criminal neighbors who dislike you; sinners/criminals are a different subject requiring higher response as sons of the Father.

▶ Watch this part (0:36)

Short answer: He says we add love because we did not have it at salvation. A noun is a person, place, or thing; 1 John 4:8 and 4:16 say God is love—so love is a person, God.
