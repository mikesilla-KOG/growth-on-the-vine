🎙From the sermon2014 Easter Sunday · Speaker not named in the recording (not identified as Pastor Kincer)▶ 0:00

In this Easter message the speaker reviews the beating and crucifixion of Jesus and the miracles at His death, then teaches “God Bless You”: the blessing of Acts 3:26 is God turning us from sin. He uses Lazarus, Zacchaeus and Mary Magdalene as examples and a chemical demonstration to show how sin hurts us and those around us, ending with a prayer.
