▶ Watch this part (0:01)

What does it mean that an apostle must be free? (“Am I not free?”)

▶ Watch this part (1:05)

Short answer: He says “free” means freeborn, free from Moses’s shadow laws (but not civil laws), free from obligations to people, “free from Satan’s kingdom,” from sin’s control and from his own ways. He says “an apostle has to be free,” and cannot be “playing games in his own life.”

🎙From the sermonTranscript of this part · Pastor David G. Kincer

▶ 1:05 And then he says what an apostle is here: “Am I not free?” Now, what he means is, I am freeborn, I’m not a slave, I’m a citizen by birth. I’m free from Moses’s shadow laws. We are not free from civil laws; we are free from the shadow laws, because Christ fulfilled the shadow laws, the examples of what was to come. And, uh, he also, the word means I’m free from obligations, obligations to people, the requirements of people. I am free from Satan’s kingdom, his kingdom of darkness. I’m free from sin’s control. I’m free from my own ways. I am free. An apostle has to be free, ain’t that right? He can’t be playing games in his own life and proclaiming to be an apostle.

Why does he say the apostles had no problems with money, sex or division?

▶ Watch this part (2:09)

Short answer: He says there is no record in the Bible or history that any apostle had a problem with greed, misuse of money, sexual immorality, extortion, intoxication, losing their temper, idolatry or being divided. He says that is because they “reached the mark,” while today’s churches have “nothing but problems.”

🎙From the sermonTranscript of this part · Pastor David G. Kincer

▶ 2:09 You will find no record in the Bible or in history recordings that any apostle had a problem with greed, the misuse of money, sexual immorality, abusing their position, extortion, intoxication, losing their temper, idolatry, or being divided. Those apostles were never divided back there. So an apostle is absolutely someone that has reached the prize of the gospel, wouldn’t you say so? Today we got nothing but problems in the churches: financially, sexually, drunkenness, intoxication going on. But you never see it recorded in the Bible with these apostles, and there were more than 12; there were about 15 that’s listed in the New Testament. Nothing. So these guys reached the mark, they lived the mark, and God used them, and so we need to know what that mark is and that prize.

What are the requirements of an apostle: a personal encounter with Jesus and changed lives?

▶ Watch this part (3:16)

Short answer: He says an apostle must have “an encounter personally with Jesus Christ” and be empowered by Him, so “you can’t just put ‘apostle’ on your name.” He says another qualification is changed lives from their preaching, and “you are the seal of my apostleship.”

🎙From the sermonTranscript of this part · Pastor David G. Kincer

▶ 3:16 He says, “Have I not seen Jesus Christ our Lord?” One of the requirements of an apostle is they got to have an encounter personally with Jesus Christ. Paul had it on the road to Damascus when Jesus appeared to him and spoke to him. The other ones had it by being with Jesus. But there has to be that personal encounter with Jesus Christ, and empowerment by Him, to be an apostle. You can’t just put “apostle” on your name. You’ve got to be free like apostles are free, and you’ve got to get that freedom through a personal encounter and delegation by Jesus Christ.

▶ 4:00 Uh, he said, “Are not you my work in the Lord?” See, another qualification of an apostle is changed lives from their preaching, from their teaching. Lives are changed, and he says you are my work in the Lord. I come out here and I preach the Word, and you have changed your life, that proves I’m an apostle. “I may not be apostle to others, yet doubtless I am to you,” because I’m the one that brought the message, and that message changed your life. I brought Christ to you, I brought His Word, I brought His truth, and you are the result. “So I am an apostle, for you are the seal of my apostleship in the Lord.” See, the seal is what the king put on a document that he sent out to his leaders or so forth, that this is a message from the king. He said you’re my proof, you’re my seal. Hallelujah. Shouldn’t we have changed lives in America, not just church membership?

▶ 7:36 Also, 2 Corinthians 12:12: “Truly the signs of an apostle were accomplished among you with all patience.” You see, an apostle has patience with the people, has patience with God. God doesn’t do things in a popcorn manner, well, all of a sudden, pow, I’m mature, pow, I’ve arrived, pow, this, pow, that. And so we’ve got to have patience with God as He works His ways in people, and we’ve got to have patience with people. They may not get the message till they hear it till the 10th time, then all of a sudden, “Oh, wow!” You don’t go putting them down and all that. They just stay steady. They’re patient with God’s people, and they’re patient with God, because it is God is the one that changes people.

▶ 8:34 Hallelujah. And so they hang in there tenaciously. Patience means you hang in there. See, you respond right no matter what’s being said to you, no matter what kind of condition you’re in, you respond right. Why? Because you’re patient. 🎙From the sermonTranscript of this part · Pastor David G. Kincer

▶ 9:36 And I’m sorry, just because I got two arms does not mean I am a body, does it? Just because I got two arms and two legs does not mean I’m a body. I need what? Two arms, two legs, a torso, and a head. I got to have everything that qualifies to be a human being, and you’ve got to have it all to be qualified to be an apostle. Amen. And if you’re not doing supernatural things, don’t give me the apostle bit, and if you’re not patient with God’s people, don’t give me that you’re an apostle bit, and if you’re not free yourself, for goodness’ sake, take the title off, because the first thing is the apostle must be free himself, not getting free.
