Why does he say we can be wrong about the second coming the way people were wrong about the first?

▶ Watch this part (0:00)

Short answer: He says the Jews and the preachers who had “their charts” at Jesus’ first coming “missed every point,” because they looked for a son of David to restore the kingdom from Rome, and “the Messiah wasn’t coming to restore the natural kingdom from Rome to them. He was coming to put a kingdom in people’s lives.” He says “we can be wrong today,” and “they did not know the Messiah was God Himself when He came the first time.”
