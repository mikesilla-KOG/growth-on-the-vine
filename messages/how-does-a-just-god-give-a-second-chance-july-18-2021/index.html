🎙From the sermonHow Does a Just God Give a Second Chance? — July 18, 2021 · Pastor David G. Kincer▶ 0:00

Pastor David G. Kincer asks how a just God gives a second chance. Because there was no law before Moses, Christ preached to the spirits in prison from Noah’s day; he suggests a just God may give a second chance to those who never understood, but urges taking it now. He criticizes faith-only teaching and lawless conservatives, calls free-salvation teaching socialism in the church, and closes with “neither do I condemn thee.”
