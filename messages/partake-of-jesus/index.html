This message shows how Moses and Israel’s journey picture Jesus: the Passover, the Red Sea as baptism, forty days of fasting and manna from heaven. Jesus is the true bread of life, and we must partake of him—not just hear or carry the word. Through Emmaus and the prodigal son, the speaker urges taking the whole bread, judgment and mercy alike, and letting the word give us life.
