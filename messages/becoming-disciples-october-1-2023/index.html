The message defines a disciple as someone disciplined who follows Jesus’ teaching and does the Father’s will. From the rich man and Lazarus it teaches that hell is real, success won’t get you to heaven, and repentance is the key; Paul’s words to Felix on righteousness, self-control and judgment are the message the world needs. It closes with Job’s steps and forgiving those who wronged us.
