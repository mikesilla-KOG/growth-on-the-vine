🎙From the sermonVictorious in Afflictions, Persecutions, Tribulations · Pastor David G. Kincer▶ 0:00

In this message Pastor David G. Kincer teaches from Isaiah 43 that God’s people are victorious in afflictions because God created, formed, redeemed and called them by name, and will be with them in the waters and the fire. He ties the waters to baptism, teaches that grace says no to ungodliness, and warns that living for Christ brings conflict. He ends with the hindrances to victory (not calling on God, impatience, forgotten offerings) and the invitation to “state your case” to God.
