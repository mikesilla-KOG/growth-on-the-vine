What does Proverbs 4:23 mean when it says to “guard your heart”?

▶ Watch this part (0:00)

Short answer: He says “guard your heart for everything you do flows from it,” and that in the chapter Solomon passes on what his father taught him: get wisdom, hold on to instruction and stay off “the path of the wicked.” He says “the path of the righteous is straight and full of light,” while evildoers “stumble in the darkness.”

Why did David pray “create in me a clean heart” in Psalm 51?

▶ Watch this part (2:23)

Short answer: He says what is in your heart is what you say, so “it’s important that we keep our heart clean.” He says David prayed Psalm 51 after adultery and killing, yet “was still a man after God’s own heart because David repented,” and “no matter what we do in this life if we are willing God will take us back.”

What is the battle between the flesh and the spirit, and which side wins?

▶ Watch this part (4:50)

Short answer: He says “the one that gets stronger is the one we feed,” so feeding the flesh will “eventually put out the spiritual side,” while feeding the spiritual side makes it “stronger and stronger.” He says coming to praise Jesus “charges your batteries,” and “if you don’t charge your batteries you’re not going to function spiritually.”

What does the bulletproof vest illustrate about guarding the heart?

▶ Watch this part (6:20)

Short answer: He describes a vest with a pocket for a card covering the heart, saying “from a spiritual standpoint” we need protection too, because “if it gets a direct hit… you’re going to die spiritually.” He says “we have to always be on guard what we take in because what we take in is what’s going to come out.”
