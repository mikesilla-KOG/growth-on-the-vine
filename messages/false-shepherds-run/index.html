What is happening in Jeremiah’s day, and why preach hope?

▶ Watch this part (0:00)

Short answer: He says Jeremiah preached when God was judging and correcting His people, with wars, captivity, famines, sickness and economic crisis. In the middle of it Jeremiah also speaks “a tremendous word of hope,” and he titles the message about the Messiah: false shepherds run and the sheep run to Jesus.
