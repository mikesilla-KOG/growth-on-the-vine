In this second part of the series Pastor David G. Kincer teaches that the death, burial and resurrection of Jesus destroyed the Devil’s power, and that a born-again believer’s new nature is not to sin. He says Satan is still released and bound to influence the world, but is limited by God, using Paul’s thorn in the flesh, pride, marriage and sexual relationships as examples. He closes by saying we do not have to wait for the rapture to experience God’s power.
