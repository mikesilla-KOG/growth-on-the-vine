What does Titus 1:15 say about the pure and the defiled?

▶ Watch this part (0:00)

Short answer: To the pure all things are pure; to the defiled and unbelieving nothing is pure—even mind and conscience are defiled. People with good conscience try to act right; a seared conscience sees no reason to act naturally right—whatever you believe is right is right to you.

How did Brown v. Board overrule “separate but equal”?

▶ Watch this part (6:09)
