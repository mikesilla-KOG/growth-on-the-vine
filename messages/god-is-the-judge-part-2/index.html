What requirements does the Bible give for a judge? (Exodus 18:21)

▶ Watch this part (0:01)

Short answer: He says Exodus 18:21 and other passages give ten requirements of a judge, starting with “fear God,” which he defines as “depart from evil and hate evil.” He adds that judges must be men of truth, “not feelings, not emotions, not opinion,” and must hate covetousness.
