Pastor David G. Kincer answers why Christ died for sinners if people can repent by conscience. Going back to Genesis like clean water at a river’s source, he shows the law of death and life, God slaying the first animal in Adam and Eve’s place, and the promised Seed who would crush Satan. Christ, slain before the foundation of the world, bought us from sin, Satan and death, and knowing Him is far better than conscience alone.
