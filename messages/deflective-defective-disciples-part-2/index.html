Do Christians need personal prophecy, and will good people go to heaven without Christ?

▶ Watch this part (0:00)

Short answer: Pastor Kincer lists prosperity, positive thinking, possibility, Purpose Driven and psychotherapy thinking, and adds personal prophecy thinking, which he says is a dime a dozen in many Spirit-filled circles. He says the claim that atheists, humanists and anyone who is good can go to heaven is lying, and that the end result of Jesus’s faith is a disciple pursuing the divine nature and doing good works.
