What does “be filled with the Spirit” mean, and who are the four recipients? (Ephesians 5:18)

▶ Watch this part (0:00)

Short answer: He says the baptism of the Holy Spirit and being filled with the Holy Spirit are “synonymous terms,” and he will look at its effect on four recipients in the New Testament, going back to the Bible and not to a revival. From Ephesians 5:18 he says wine changes your personality, and the Holy Spirit “will change the way you behave, the way you speak.”

🎙From the sermonTranscript of this part · Pastor David G. Kincer

▶ 0:00 We’re going to continue with the third promise. Remember, we showed you the first three promises, and the last one was the baptism of the Holy Ghost. God promised that, and it plays an integral part in us partaking of the divine nature. And this third promise is so vast and broad I have to bring it in segments, and so instead of this week showing you it was a promise and all that, we’re going to look a little further into this baptism of the Holy Ghost, and we’re going to look at it this way: the baptism of the Holy Spirit and its effect on four recipients. I’m going to bring you four recipients in the New Testament of the baptism of the Holy Ghost, which is the baptism of the Holy Spirit, which is being filled with the Holy Spirit. They’re all synonymous terms.

▶ 1:01 Okay, and we’re not going to go back to Azusa Street, we’re not going to go back to the 1968 charismatic revival and get four recipients out of there. We’re going to go back to the Bible. Ephesians 5:18 says, “Do not be drunk with wine, wherein is excess, but be filled with the Spirit.” You know, if Christians would not twist that Scripture to say, “Well, what he means is, if I drink and I don’t get intoxicated, I’m fine.” It isn’t saying that at all. What it really says: “Be not drunk with wine, wherein is excess.” Excess means it will lead you to wrong behavior, loose behavior. Wine will distort your judgment, alcohol will, and so you can’t use that to justify drinking at all. But his comparison is with the wine because wine changes your personality, am I right? Yes. Yeah, it affects you. So he illustrates it with the wine because the Holy Spirit (be filled, be baptized with the Holy Spirit) will affect you. It will change the way you behave, the way you speak, and so all of us are supposed to be baptized with the Holy Ghost.
