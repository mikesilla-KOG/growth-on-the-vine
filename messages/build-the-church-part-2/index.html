Short answer: He says grace is more than “just favor”: it shows in “the divine influence upon your life, divine enablement to live a lifestyle different than you lived before.” He says the American church has done members “a disservice” by teaching that God favors you no matter what you do.

🎙From the sermonTranscript of this part · Pastor David G. Kincer

▶ 8:33 Verse three of 1 Corinthians 1: “Grace be to you and peace from God our Father and from the Lord Jesus Christ. I thank my God always on your behalf for the grace of God given you by Jesus Christ.” And this is where the American church has done the church members a disservice. They keep telling them grace is just favor, God just favors you no matter what you do, He favors you. But it’s much more than that. He favors you, and it shows in a result of the divine influence upon your life, divine enablement to live a lifestyle different than you lived before. That’s the full measure of grace. That’s the whole purpose of grace: that He favors you, He loves you, He’s going to work with you so that people will see that there’s a divine influence, a divine enablement, that changed your life from this way to His way.
