In this message Pastor David G. Kincer teaches from Isaiah 34 and Revelation 6 about God’s dealing with the Gentiles: the ungodly and secular humanists. He argues that the stars falling and the sun darkening are not allegory, that Esau and the ten virgins show a door that closes, and that God’s purpose in judgment is to win the nations to Christ. He closes with two family stories to show how far parents go for their children, and says God wants even his opponents to live.
