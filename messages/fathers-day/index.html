In this Father’s Day message a guest speaker compares the birth of a baby with being born again, saying a baby has only two options, to be born and live or to die, and the same is true spiritually. He then follows Israel out of Egypt: Satan going after new believers through Pharaoh and the midwives, the lamb’s blood on the doorposts, and the hindrances after deliverance, including complaining, the flesh, emotions and fear. He closes with Jonah 2:9, “Salvation is of the Lord.”
