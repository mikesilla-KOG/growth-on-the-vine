What is the difference between a leader’s qualifications and a leader’s essentials?

▶ Watch this part (0:57)

Short answer: He says the qualifications in 1 Timothy 3 and Titus 1 (such as running your own home and being married to one spouse) are different from “the basic things that a leader must have.” He says having the qualifications “does not mean you have the basics of leadership.”

🎙From the sermonTranscript of this part · Pastor David G. Kincer

▶ 0:57 Timothy is a young leader. He’s a young guy coming up through the ranks, okay, and Paul is admonishing the congregation how you should treat a young leader. All right, and so I’m going to call this a young leader’s essentials. Now this is different from the qualifications of a leader found in 1 Timothy 3 and Titus chapter 1. You can’t even be a leader if you don’t qualify. Okay, you got to be able to run your own home, all, you got to be the, you can only be married to one spouse, you can’t take much wine and all that stuff. That’s the qualifications. But here are the basic things that a leader must have to be a leader. Just because you got the qualifications found in 1 Timothy and Titus does not mean you have the basics of leadership. Do you understand that? Yeah, see, all right.
