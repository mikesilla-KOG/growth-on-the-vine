In this message a guest speaker at Vine Cluster Church continues his study of Esther in chapters 8–10 as a parable of the new covenant. He teaches that the new covenant has authority over the old, that the ceremonial “shadow laws” point to Jesus while the moral law stays, and that Jesus gives his church power. He closes with the judgment on Jerusalem in AD 70 and a call to stand like Mordecai today.

Why does God hide things from “the wise and learned” and reveal them to little children? (Matthew 11:25)
