More questions this message answers

Short answers drawn only from what he says in this message, each with the moment in the video.

Why does he stress “may have peace” in John 16:33?

Because it is your decision whether you accept and follow him; peace takes a commitment, but trouble in the world is certain.

▶ Watch at 0:00

What does simplicity mean here?

Nothing complicated—when people complicate Jesus or the Bible it is a sign they don’t know them.

▶ Watch at 0:53

Is abortion new?

No—he says in the Roman Empire women would throw a delivered child in a river or use chemicals to abort.

▶ Watch at 2:51

Who besides Jews died in the Nazi camps, according to the sermon?

He says about seven million others, mostly Slavs—Czechoslovakians, Poles and Russians—whom Hitler declared inferior.

▶ Watch at 6:50

Did America help Israel in 1948?

He says America accepted them as a nation but did not help, and Truman’s embargo blocked arms sales.

▶ Watch at 13:15

Does the church force people to join or stay?

No—he says no one is physically forcing them to join or forbidding them to leave, unlike Muslims and other religions.

▶ Watch at 15:13

Should Christians refuse to judge?

He says “don’t judge” is foolish; keeping God’s rules and making judgments is our wisdom before the nations.

▶ Watch at 18:24

Is drunkenness serious?

He says the Bible calls drunkenness a hell sin, and says not even to look on the wine when it is red in the cup.

▶ Watch at 19:17

Does he call forced redistribution stealing?

He says taking something from someone without their permission is stealing, and applies it to promises of making the rich pay for everything.

▶ Watch at 21:12

Were the creation days long ages?

He says the evening and the morning were the first day as the earth rotated, and God repeats that he made heaven and earth in six days.
