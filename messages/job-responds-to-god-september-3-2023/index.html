The final message in the Job series shows how Job responded when God spoke from the whirlwind: he acknowledged God can do everything, admitted he spoke what he didn’t understand, heard God, and repented in dust and ashes. When Job prayed for his friends, God restored him double; we too must confess, hate evil, forgive and change our behavior.
