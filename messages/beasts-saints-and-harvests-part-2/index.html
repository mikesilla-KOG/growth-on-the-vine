Who does not worship the beast? (Revelation 13:8, 14:12)

▶ Watch this part (0:01)

Short answer: He says “all worship the beast and Satan, except some people,” “those in the Book of Life,” the ones “that got born again.” He says they “didn’t join a church” or get a baptismal certificate; they “were touched by God.” He says churches “just put people on the roll book,” Jesus said “you got to be born again of the word and the Spirit,” and the saints’ faith is “in Jesus Christ, not in a church, not in a person, not in a minister.”
