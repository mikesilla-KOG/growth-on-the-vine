What does he mean that God is who the Bible says he is? (Philippians 4:13, Romans 8:37)

▶ Watch this part (0:01)

Short answer: He says “God is who the Bible says he is, not who you say he is,” and “I don’t care about your opinion.” He says believers “need to get out of your faith level and into the Bible level,” and that “I’m who the Bible says I am, not what some mega church minister says I am.” He cites “I can do all things through Christ” and “more than conquerors.”

What does he say about positional and conditional righteousness? (Luke 1:75)

▶ Watch this part (6:43)

Short answer: He says most Christians in America “are only righteous positionally by the blood,” and “God wants it to be a condition, not just a position.” He says “the position is important while you’re growing,” but “the condition is the goal,” and that without it “they live weak Christian lives.”
