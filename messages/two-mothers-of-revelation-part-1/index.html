He says the woman of Revelation 12 is a spiritual mother, the church, whose children overcome Satan and are persecuted by him, then turns to the second woman of Revelation 17, whom he describes as people who use Christ’s name but do not follow him, serving God their own way. He teaches that Revelation is a spiritual illustration, not a list of natural kingdoms, and speaks about the beast, the seven mountains and spiritual fornication.
