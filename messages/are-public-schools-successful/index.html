In “Are Public Schools Successful?”, Pastor David G. Kincer traces the rise of public education in America, the Protestants’ early reservations, and the beliefs of the groups he says shaped government schools. He says the results have not met the schools’ goals, and he answers with Proverbs 22:6, Proverbs 27:12, Hosea 4:6 and Proverbs 4:7: train children in the way they should go, make them prudent, and make understanding the supreme thing. He calls for change, competition, and home, private and Christian schools as options.

Are public schools the best choice for our children?

▶ Watch this part (0:00)

Short answer: He asks whether public schools are successful and says they are now government schools, where the government, the humanists and the teachers unions decide what is taught. He says early-1800s reformers argued tax-funded schools would create good citizens and prevent crime and poverty.

Asia went communist, Europe went socialist, and they’re trying to get America.
