What is scene one?

Short answer: The judgment seat—Pilate finds no guilt, the crowd cries “Crucify him,” and Pilate washes his hands.

🎙From the sermonTranscript of this part · Unidentified narrator and performers (Easter drama)

▶ 0:06 Scene one, the judgement seat before a large crowd in Jerusalem. Jesus stands bound and scourged before Pilate. Pilate questioned Jesus, “Are you the king of the Jews?” Jesus answered that his kingdom is not of this world. Pilate declared to the crowd that he found no guilt in him. Whom shall I release? Barabbas the bandit or Jesus called the Christ? Barabbas! Barabbas! What shall I do with Jesus? Crucify him! Crucify him! Pilate asked what evil he had done, but they shouted louder. The crowd cried out again for crucifixion. Pilate said he found no guilt, but they insisted he must die.
