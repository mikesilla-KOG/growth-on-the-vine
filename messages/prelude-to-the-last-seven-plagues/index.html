What are the seven last plagues, and where do the angels come from? (Revelation 15:1)

▶ Watch this part (0:00)

Short answer: He says Revelation 15:1 shows seven angels with the seven last plagues, “filled with God’s wrath, God’s anger, God’s punishment,” coming out of the temple in heaven.
