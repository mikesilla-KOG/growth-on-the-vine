What are the three kinds of laws in the Old Testament?

▶ Watch this part (0:00)

Short answer: He says there are civil laws (like “Thou shalt not murder”), which carry a punishment; spiritual laws (like seek the Lord and pray); and shadow laws. He says critics are “confused about the difference between civil, spiritual, and shadow laws.”

That’s a civil law and it has a punishment if you break it. Then there are spiritual laws. Seek the Lord your God and you will find him. Pray, you know, come before him and sing. See that’s spiritual laws. And then there is shadow laws. As we heard earlier, shadow laws. Say the word shadow. Yeah. And this is what these people are confused about the difference between civil, spiritual, and shadow laws.
