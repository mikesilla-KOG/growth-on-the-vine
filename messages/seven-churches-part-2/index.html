Why does Jesus say “I know your works” to every church, and what does he praise in Ephesus? (Revelation 2:1-3)

▶ Watch this part (0:00)

Short answer: He says all seven churches hear “I know your works,” which “starts right off shattering American theology.” He says grace and works go together, and that Ephesus is praised for testing false apostles, patient labor and persevering: “you fall down, you get back up.”
