What are the marks of a real prophet, and does it matter what other preachers say?

▶ Watch this part (0:00)

Short answer: He says the first mark is that “God did speak to them. God did send them,” and the second that “they did stand in His counsel”: they were people of the Word who read, studied and got into teaching where the Spirit revealed the Scriptures. He says they cause people to hear God’s words, and it does not matter what others say, “it’s what the Bible says.” When people say he thinks he is right and everyone else is wrong, he says, “I’m just telling you what the Bible says.”

🎙From the sermonTranscript of this part · Pastor David G. Kincer

▶ 0:00 I want to give you the marks of a real prophet. They’re right here. Characteristic number one: God did speak to them. God did send them. Can I hear an amen? It’s right here, the opposite of the false. Characteristic number two: they did stand in His counsel. They were people of the Word. They found time to read and study the Word and get in teaching where the Word was being taught, not some book out of the Bible bookstore, but the Scriptures were being taught by the Holy Spirit revealing them. That’s the kind of people that are real prophets. “And they caused my people to hear my words,” not their words. See, it doesn’t matter what Copeland says, it doesn’t matter what the pope says, it’s what the Bible says.

▶ 1:11 Hallelujah. And I hear this all the time: “Well, you think you’re right and everybody else is wrong.” I didn’t say that. I’m just telling you what the Bible says. I’m not telling you what I say, I’m not telling you what I believe, I’m telling you what the Bible says. They probably put you on a guilt trip. “Well, how can all these people...” I hear this too: “How can all these people be wrong and you’re right?” I’m not telling you I’m right, I’m telling you what the Bible says. You show me in the Bible where you are right, and they can’t. They just believe some book, some tape, some CD, some guy off the TV.
