In “Magnifical House Reviewed,” Pastor David G. Kincer revisits 1 Chronicles 22:5’s exceeding magnifical house and applies it to the church as living stones (1 Peter 2:5)—strong like a three-fold cord when united with word and Jesus. And we will walk in His paths. Why? Because the Holy Ghost would convict us. The Holy Ghost will empower us to obey the word, not just hear it. For out of Zion—and Hebrews 12 tells us Zion is the church, and Zion is the new city of Jerusalem, the church—out of the church will go forth the law—[unclear] law, [unclear] law, and spiritual law—and the word of the Lord, what He commanded us to teach, from Jerusalem, from the church. So the word is supposed to go from the church, is supposed to be in the church—a living word, a powerful word from the Holy Spirit. And then look at the results when we do these things: He will judge among the nations, He will correct many people, they will beat their swords into plowshares and their spears into pruning hooks. Nation shall not lift up sword against nation, neither shall they learn war anymore. And I submit to you: has this ever been fulfilled? No. Well, why do you think the rapture is some kind of magical answer when this is just one of the things in the Bible that has not been fulfilled? But when we get our act together and become

▶ 22:59 a magnifical church that He’s building in these last days, He will have no problem bringing this to pass. He is Jesus and He is powerful. Hallelujah. And so it pays us to let the Holy Spirit come, the presence of God come, and teach us His ways so that we also have that presence of God motivating us to walk in His paths. These four things are necessary. And we don’t have a church service without any of them. But the most important part of these four things of prayer, praise and worship and thanksgiving and blessing and teaching the word—the most important part is His presence. The Bible says without Him we can do nothing, and the American church on the most part is trying to do this thing without Him.
