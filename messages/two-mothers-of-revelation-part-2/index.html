What does the Bible mean by Babylon, the “destroying mountain”?

▶ Watch this part (0:00)

Short answer: He says Babylon is called a destroying mountain in Jeremiah: a nation, a group of people destroying other people for all the evil done in Zion, which he says is the church, the people of God. He says they lead astray not just Christians but people who do not believe in Jesus, and that the shepherds in Jeremiah 50:6 lead people astray on the mountains, serving God the way they should not, so that they forget their resting place, Jesus Christ.

🎙From the sermonTranscript of this part · Pastor David G. Kincer

▶ 0:00 Shepherds cause my people, in Jeremiah 50:6, to go astray on the what? Mountains. And what are they doing on the mountains? Serving God the way they shouldn’t be serving. And they forget their resting place. Who is our resting place? Jesus Christ. Jeremiah 51:24: “I’m against Babylon, O destroying mountain.” [Editor’s note: the words “destroying mountain” are in Jeremiah 51:25; verse 24 speaks of the evil done in Zion.] So Babylon is referred to as a what? Destroying mountain. A nation, a group of people that are destroying other people for all the evil done in Zion. Zion is the church, the people of God, and destroying all the earth. They don’t just lead Christians astray and people who believe in Jesus astray; they lead people who don’t believe in Jesus astray. Are you there? Hallelujah.
