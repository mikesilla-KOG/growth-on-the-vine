🎙From the sermonNot a Spirit of Fear 07/07/2019 · Pastor David G. Kincer▶ 0:00

Pastor David G. Kincer teaches that God has not given us a spirit of fear but of power, love and a sound mind. Drawing on his own years repairing televisions, he compares God’s power with electricity: present since creation but useless until applied, and dangerous outside its rules. Knowledge without power is self-destruction; application brings abundant life.
