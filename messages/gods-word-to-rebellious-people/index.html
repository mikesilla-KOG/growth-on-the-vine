What does Isaiah 29 say about God punishing His people and their opponents, and honoring Him with lips only? (Isaiah 29:1-13)

▶ Watch this part (0:00)

Short answer: He says Isaiah 29 gives “a word about God punishing the city of God, the people of God, Jerusalem, and her opponents,” both those God used to correct His people and those who just oppose God and His people. He says those who fight God and His church are like a man who dreams he eats but wakes up still hungry, and that we can say we are believers while “the heart, the mind, the will, our actions, our attitude is removed far from God.”
