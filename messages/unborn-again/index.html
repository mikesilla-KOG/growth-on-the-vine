What does natural growth from a seed teach about spiritual growth?

▶ Watch this part (0:00)

Short answer: He says “the Bible always uses natural illustrations to help us understand the spiritual.” Every seed has an embryo that grows “into the image that God designed it to grow in,” and in the spirit “the word of God is what’s going to start that seed in us.”
