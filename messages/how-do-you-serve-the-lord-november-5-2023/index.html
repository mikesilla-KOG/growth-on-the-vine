🎙From the sermonHow do you serve the Lord? 🤲 - November 5, 2023 · Unidentified speaker (not clearly Pastor Kincer)▶ 0:00

In the series on serving God in every season, the message asks how we serve when the miracles stop. Joshua chose to serve the Lord, Solomon neglected his relationship, Mary chose the good part, and those Jesus healed were told to go home, serve and sin no more. It calls for daily bread, wisdom to build our homes, love in marriage, forgiving ourselves and others, and passing on Christlike character.
