What is the Bible definition of brotherly love?

▶ Watch this part (0:36)

Short answer: He says brotherly love is to love the true church, its membership, the true believer, and that it is confirmation of being born again—a change from wrong behavior and thinking to right by the Scriptures. He reads 1 Peter 1:22–23 about purifying souls in obeying the truth unto genuine brotherly love, loving one another fervently with a pure heart.

Brotherly love is confirmation of being born again—is going to be our first look at brotherly love. It’s a confirmation of being born again. Being born again, not of corruptible seed but incorruptible, by the word of God.
