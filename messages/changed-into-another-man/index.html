From 1 Samuel 10:6, Pastor David G. Kincer teaches that when the Spirit of the Lord came upon Saul, God gave him another heart and turned him into another man, and that this transformation, more than prophecy or spiritual gifts, is God’s goal for every believer. He explains the new heart as a new mind, will and emotions, and illustrates it with Joshua and Caleb, Joseph, Barnabas, Esther and his own father.
