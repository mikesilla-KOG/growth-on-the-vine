What message will usher in the end time? (Matthew 24:14; Luke 24:47)

▶ Watch this part (0:00)

Short answer: He says the gospel of the kingdom shall be preached to all nations “as a witness,” and Jesus said repentance and remission of sins should be preached in His name to all nations. He says that message, not prosperity, the second coming, positive thinking, personal prophecy or poverty, is what will usher in the end time.
