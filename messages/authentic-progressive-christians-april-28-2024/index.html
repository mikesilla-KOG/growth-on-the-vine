🎙From the sermonAuthentic Progressive Christians - April 28, 2024 · Unidentified speaker (not clearly Pastor Kincer)▶ 0:00

Real progress in Christ’s kingdom is the reverse of the world’s: the last are first and the great serve. The authentic progressive Christian lets the world decrease and Christ increase, flees immorality and drunkenness, refuses dishonest trade with the cross, and lets God break him like a war horse for endurance. Those who wait on the Lord are renewed, as Peter and Cornelius found.
