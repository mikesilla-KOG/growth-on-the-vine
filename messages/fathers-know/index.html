Who are the “fathers” in 1 John 2:13-14, and what does it mean that they know Him from the beginning?

▶ Watch this part (0:00)

Short answer: He says the Bible repeats itself in 1 John 2:13-14 because “when the Bible repeats itself, it means it’s important.” He says fathers “not only understand with the mind, but they have a personal relationship and they keep His commandments,” and that they understand Jesus “existed at the beginning” and “was God from the very beginning of the world.”

🎙From the sermonTranscript of this part · Pastor David G. Kincer

▶ 0:00 In 1 John 2:13 and 14 he says the same thing. He repeats himself, and when the Bible repeats itself, it means it’s important. “I write unto you, fathers, because you have known Him from the beginning.” Now we know from past sessions that the word “know” means to understand, okay, to be aware of, to recognize something. We recognize God, we get knowledge of God, but we also know from the Bible that knowing God is an intimate relationship. It’s used as an intimate, personal relationship. And we also know that by this do you know Him: if you keep His commandments. So the fathers know Him. They not only understand with the mind, but they have a personal relationship and they keep His commandments. That is from the beginning.

▶ 1:08 Now here Paul, John is saying that Jesus was at the beginning. Okay, He wasn’t just, when He came, was born of a virgin, but He existed at the beginning, and fathers understand that. They have a personal encounter with that, and they obey that Jesus that was at the beginning. So fathers know Jesus was God. See, the father stage clearly understands that Jesus was God from the very beginning of the world. All right, John chapter 1, verse 1 through 14. We find in John 1:1 through 14 that “in the beginning was the Word.” Now John is going to lay this out very clearly: “In the beginning was the Word, the Word was with God.”
