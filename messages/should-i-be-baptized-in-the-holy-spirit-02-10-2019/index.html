Is the baptism of the Holy Ghost for today?

▶ Watch this part (0:00)

Short answer: Yes. The Bible says repent and be baptized and you shall receive the gift of the Holy Ghost, poured out on all flesh, not some flesh at a certain time.

What is the proof of being filled?

▶ Watch this part (0:55)

Short answer: The power to be a witness for Jesus, live for Him and be a light; you may speak in tongues or prophesy, but the real power is what Job had: to serve God no matter what happens.
