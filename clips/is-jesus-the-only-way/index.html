Is Jesus really the only way?

🎙Watch the clipPastor David G. Kincer · “Division Concluded Part 2”▶ 8:05

💡 Short answerYes. Jesus is the only way. No one comes to the Father except through Him.

🔎 WhyPastor Kincer says the Bible calls Jesus the only way, the only truth and the only life, so we can’t change this gospel. Real unity is around Jesus, His Word and the cross.
