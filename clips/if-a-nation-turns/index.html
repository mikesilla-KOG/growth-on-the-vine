Clip · Saving a Nation

What if a nation turns back to God?

What if a nation turns back to God? Jeremiah shows that repentance matters even when judgment has been pronounced.

Key Takeaway

God warns before judgment, and a nation that turns from evil can receive mercy instead of the destruction it was headed toward.

Key Scripture

Jeremiah 18:8; Jeremiah 12:17 — “If that nation against whom I have spoken turns from its evil, I will relent of the disaster that I thought to bring upon it. But if they do not obey, I will utterly pluck up and destroy that nation, says the LORD.”

Jeremiah 18:8 says, “If that nation against whom I have spoken turns from its evil, I will relent of the disaster that I thought to bring upon it.” Pastor David G. Kincer explains that God warns before judgment and that turning from evil to Bible morality brings a people under His protection.

If that nation against whom I have spoken turns from its evil, I will relent of the disaster that I thought to bring upon it. But if they do not obey, I will utterly pluck up and destroy that nation, says the LORD.— Jeremiah 18:8; Jeremiah 12:17 (NKJV)

Scripture

Jeremiah 18:8; Jeremiah 12:17 (NKJV) — “If that nation against whom I have spoken turns from its evil, I will relent of the disaster that I thought to bring upon it. But if they do not obey, I will utterly pluck up and destroy that nation, says the LORD.”

Plain language: hear God’s Word, turn from evil, and let Scripture do its work in your life and community.

Short transcript

Jeremiah 18:8 says, “If that nation, against whom I have pronounced, turn from their evil, I will repent of the evil that I thought to do unto them.” Pastor Kincer says God warns before He judges. If a nation turns from its evil ways and back to Bible morality, God can relent from the judgment He planned.
