What if a nation turns back to God?

🎙Watch the clipPastor David G. Kincer · “Saving a Nation”▶ 21:54

💡 Short answerGod says He will relent from the judgment He planned. Pastor Kincer adds that Bible morality is an umbrella of protection when God judges.

🔎 WhyJeremiah shows both sides: a nation that won’t obey is plucked up, but one that turns from its evil is spared. That is why, Pastor Kincer says, we need to get the Word out.

Short transcript

Jeremiah 18:8 says, “If that nation, against whom I have pronounced, turn from their evil, I will repent of the evil that I thought to do unto them.” Pastor Kincer says God warns before He judges. If a nation turns from its evil ways and back to Bible morality, God can relent from the judgment He planned.
