Clip · Saving a Nation

Can the Word still turn a nation?

💡 Short answerYes. Pastor Kincer says that if the church gets the message out, the Word does the work — it convicts and convinces people.

🔎 WhyPastor Kincer points to God’s promise that His word will not return void. He says it is the Word — not a denomination — that must get out, and the turning of a nation starts with the church.

Our short summary of this clip — not the preacher’s exact words.

Short transcript

Can the Word still turn a nation? Isaiah says God’s Word will not return unto Him void, but it will accomplish what He pleases. Pastor Kincer says it is the Word that must get out: it convicts and convinces people, and the turning of a nation starts with the church getting beyond its four walls.
