Clip · Saving a Nation

Can the Word still turn a nation?

Can the Word still turn a nation? God’s Word does the work when the church faithfully gets it beyond its four walls.

Key Takeaway

The Word itself convicts and convinces people; the turning of a nation starts with the church speaking Scripture beyond its walls.

Key Scripture

Isaiah 55:11 — “So shall my word be that goeth forth out of my mouth: it shall not return unto me void, but it shall accomplish that which I please, and it shall prosper in the thing whereto I sent it.”

Isaiah 55:11 promises, “So shall my word be that goeth forth out of my mouth: it shall not return unto me void, but it shall accomplish that which I please.” Pastor David G. Kincer says the Word itself convicts and convinces people, and that the turning of a nation starts with the church getting God’s Word out.

Short transcript

Can the Word still turn a nation? Isaiah says God’s Word will not return unto Him void, but it will accomplish what He pleases. Pastor Kincer says it is the Word that must get out: it convicts and convinces people, and the turning of a nation starts with the church getting beyond its four walls.
