What makes a real father?

🎙Watch the clipPastor David G. Kincer · “Division Concluded Part 1”▶ 15:01

💡 Short answerA real father gives life and then guides his child all the way to maturity. Anybody can have kids, but not anybody does that.

🔎 WhyPastor Kincer says there are 10,000 tutors talking, but not many fathers who set aside the time and labor to raise a child up in every stage to be well-balanced, serving God and an asset to the community.

Our short summary of this clip — not the preacher’s exact words.
