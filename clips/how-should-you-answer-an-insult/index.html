How should you answer an insult?

🎙Watch the clipPastor David G. Kincer · “Division Concluded Part 1”▶ 10:17

💡 Short answerGive good words back. When you’re spoken evil of, answer with kindness and goodness.

🔎 WhyPastor Kincer says Paul was reviled and blessed in return, and that is the sign of a real minister and a real believer who is thinking right, even when verbally abused.
