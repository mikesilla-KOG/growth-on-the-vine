Can God really change a person?

🎙Watch the clipPastor David G. Kincer · “Division Part 2”▶ 5:25

💡 Short answerYes. When Pastor Kincer surrendered at the altar, God’s Spirit made him a changed person.

🔎 WhyHe tells his own testimony: he was saved, then baptized, and he left church that night in love with everybody, with no enemies.
