Noise Without Love

Watch on YouTube Shorts ↗

If I speak with the tongues of men and of angels, but have not love — I’m just noise. A clanging cymbal.

Key Takeaway

Speech without love is just noise — a clanging cymbal. Love is the more excellent way.

Key Scripture

1 Corinthians 13:1 — If I speak with the tongues of men and of angels, but have not love, I am a noisy gong or a clanging cymbal.

In A More Excellent Way, Pastor David G. Kincer opens 1 Corinthians 13 where Paul left off in chapter 12: desire the best gifts… and I will show you a more excellent way. Then Paul says:

If I speak with the tongues of men and of angels, but have not love, I am a noisy gong or a clanging cymbal. — 1 Corinthians 13:1

Languages of people. Languages of angels. Impressive. But without love, Paul says it’s worthless racket — the kind of noise that makes you cover your ears and ask, “What’s the point?”

Love isn’t the soft add-on after the gifts. Love is the more excellent way.

Ask yourself: Am I impressive — or am I loving?

Scripture

1 Corinthians 12:31 — Desire the best gifts… and yet I show you a more excellent way.
1 Corinthians 13:1 — Tongues of men and angels without love = a noisy gong or a clanging cymbal.

Plain language: giftedness without love is empty noise. Love is the more excellent way Paul points to.
