Is God only for emergencies?

🎙Watch the clipPastor David G. Kincer · “Division Part 2”▶ 16:04

💡 Short answerNo. Pastor Kincer says God is not just for emergencies — we need Him every day of our life.

🔎 WhyHe recalls that after 9/11 the churches filled with people praying, then emptied again in about 60 days, because people think God is only good for an immediate necessity. In the hospital, he says, he didn’t need a healer to come in — he needed the Healer, Jesus.
