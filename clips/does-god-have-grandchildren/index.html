Clip · Division Part 2

Does God have grandchildren?

Does God have grandchildren? Being raised in a church family is not the same as being born again yourself.

To see the full message, click here

Key Takeaway

“My granddad was a Baptist, my dad was a Baptist, I’m a Baptist — don’t bug me.” Pastor Kincer says God has no grandchildren and no stepchildren, only children. Each of us has to be born again and meet Him for ourselves.

Key Scripture

John 3:3 — “Most assuredly, I say to you, unless one is born again, he cannot see the kingdom of God.”
