Love Doesn't Demand Its Own Way

Love doesn’t say “my way or the highway.” It demands the right way — the truth — and keeps no record of wrongs.

Key Takeaway

Love does not demand its own way; it keeps no record of wrongs.

Key Scripture

1 Corinthians 13:4–7 — Love is patient and kind… it does not insist on its own way… it keeps no record of wrongs.

Paul doesn’t leave love vague. In 1 Corinthians 13:4–7 he defines it — patient, kind, not proud, not rude — and then the line that lands at home and church:

Love does not seek its own… Love keeps no record of wrongs. — 1 Corinthians 13:5 (sermon reading)

Love does not demand its own way in the family, the church, or the community. It demands the right way — the truth. And it isn’t touchy; it keeps no ledger of hurts.

Ask yourself: Am I demanding my way — or the right way?

Scripture

1 Corinthians 13:4–7 — Love is patient and kind; it does not envy or boast; it is not arrogant or rude; it does not insist on its own way; it is not irritable; it keeps no record of wrongs; it rejoices with the truth; it bears, believes, hopes, and endures all things.

Plain language: love won’t bully the room into “my way,” and it won’t keep score of wrongs.
