Short transcript

My little children, Galatians 4:19, of whom I travail and birth again until what be formed in you, Christ. To form Christ in you so that you are a light to the world. A city set on a hill that everybody can see that imitates Jesus Christ. I don't know about you, but when I read that verse as a young Christian man, that just really gave me tremendous hope because I was struggling living for Christ. And then these scriptures, God is in me and He's shaping me into Himself. So I could accept where I was at while I was pursuing the goal.
