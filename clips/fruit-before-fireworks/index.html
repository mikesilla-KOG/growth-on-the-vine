Fruit Before Fireworks

Love is the first fruit of the Spirit and the peak of divine character. Don’t chase manifestations without becoming like God.

Key Takeaway

Character (love) comes before chasing spiritual fireworks.

Key Scripture

Galatians 5:22; 1 John 4:8; 1 Corinthians 13:8 — But the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace… God is love. Love never fails.

Paul isn’t anti-gifts. He’s saying you don’t get lasting spiritual manifestations by chasing them — you get them by becoming like God.

But the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace…
God is love.
Love never fails. — Galatians 5:22 · 1 John 4:8 · 1 Corinthians 13:8

Love is the first fruit of the Spirit and the peak of divine character (2 Peter 1). Character first. Then the fireworks.

Ask yourself: Am I chasing manifestations — or becoming love?

Scripture

Galatians 5:22 — The fruit of the Spirit is love… (love listed first).
1 John 4:8 — God is love.
1 Corinthians 13:8 — Love never fails.

Plain language: don’t chase spiritual fireworks without the fruit. Love is who God is — and how lasting manifestations come.
