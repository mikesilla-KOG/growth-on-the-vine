Can a divided home still stand?

Can a divided home still stand? Jesus said a house divided against itself shall not stand.

Key Takeaway

A husband and wife need to stand together in the same truth and moral standards, showing their children Jesus by example, or the home will not stand.

Key Scripture

Matthew 12:25 — “And Jesus knew their thoughts, and said unto them, Every kingdom divided against itself is brought to desolation; and every city or house divided against itself shall not stand.”

Jesus said, “Every kingdom divided against itself is brought to desolation; and every city or house divided against itself shall not stand” (Matthew 12:25). Pastor David G. Kincer says husband and wife must stand together in the same truth and show their children Jesus by their lives, or the home will not stand.

Scripture

Matthew 12:25 (KJV) — “And Jesus knew their thoughts, and said unto them, Every kingdom divided against itself is brought to desolation; and every city or house divided against itself shall not stand.”

Plain language: let God’s Word unite us, keep Christ at the center, and live out the same truth together.

Short transcript

Jesus said every kingdom, city and house divided against itself shall not stand (Matthew 12:25). Pastor Kincer says “stand” is a military word: an army that isn’t united loses the war. In the home, husband and wife must stand together with the same truth and moral standards, showing their children Jesus by their lives. When they go two different ways, conflict comes, and the kids can’t wait to get out.
