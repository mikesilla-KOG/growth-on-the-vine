Is following Jesus foolish?

🎙Watch the clipPastor David G. Kincer · “Division Concluded Part 1”▶ 4:47

💡 Short answerTo the world it looks foolish, but putting Christ first is the wisest choice you can make.

🔎 WhyPastor Kincer points to Peter and John leaving a good fishing business to preach. The world calls that crazy, but believers are “fools for Christ’s sake” because they put first what He puts first.
