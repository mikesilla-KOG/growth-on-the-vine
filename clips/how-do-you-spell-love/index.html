How do you spell love?

🎙Watch the clipPastor David G. Kincer · “Division Part 2”▶ 21:18

💡 Short answerT-I-M-E. If you say you love God, you’ll spend time with Him.

🔎 WhyPastor Kincer says sports and recreation don’t give us power. Whatever we really love, we make time for, and that includes God.
