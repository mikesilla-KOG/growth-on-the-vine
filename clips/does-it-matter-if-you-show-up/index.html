Short transcript

You see, I come to church on Friday night because I care. See, I care. I don't have any speaking part. I don't have any singing part. But as I was telling someone last week, the power of one member just being present, God uses to touch people's lives. I said, you don't understand the power you have just by being in the assembly. And you don't understand the power you have when you lift your hands and you just worship because the Bible says so. You don't understand what God can do through a simple obedience to his word. See, he's the one that causes the growth. It's not you.
