Perfect Means Mature

When Jesus says “be perfect,” He isn’t demanding a flawless life — He’s calling you into spiritual maturity and completeness.

Key Takeaway

“Be perfect” means grow into maturity — not never mess up.

Key Scripture

Matthew 5:48; James 1:4 — Be ye therefore perfect, even as your Father which is in heaven is perfect. … that ye may be perfect and entire, wanting nothing.

Jesus said, “Be ye therefore perfect.” A lot of people hear that and shut down — “You can’t be perfect; we all sin.” They thought perfect meant a life with no flaws.

Be ye therefore perfect, even as your Father which is in heaven is perfect. — Matthew 5:48

No — it means spiritual maturity, spiritual completeness. James 1:4: let patience have her perfect work that you may be perfect and entire, lacking nothing. God is taking His people somewhere.

Ask yourself: Am I growing up — or staying a spiritual child?

Scripture

Matthew 5:48 — Be ye therefore perfect, even as your Father in heaven is perfect.
James 1:4 — Let patience have her perfect work, that ye may be perfect and entire, wanting nothing.
Hebrews 5:14 — Strong meat belongs to those who are mature.

Plain language: “perfect” here means mature and complete — not never mess up. Grow up.
