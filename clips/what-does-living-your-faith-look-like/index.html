What does living your faith look like?

🎙Watch the clipPastor David G. Kincer · “Division Concluded Part 2”▶ 9:36

💡 Short answerIt looks like living what you believe in public and giving Jesus the credit, the way Paul did.

🔎 WhyPastor Kincer points to Tim Tebow: he stands up for the Bible’s moral standards, so when he bows he isn’t putting on an act, and on TV the first thing he does is praise his Lord and Savior Jesus Christ.

Short transcript

And this is the God that Tebow talks about. I was asking one with Tebow on their t-shirt. Do you have the character of Tebow? Tebow stands up for the Bible, the moral standards of the Bible. So he has a right when he Tebows. He's not putting on an act. He is bowing to the King of his life. And I love it. Simply for living for Christ.
